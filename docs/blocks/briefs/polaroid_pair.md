# Polaroid pair

Purpose: Two captioned prints for a paired visual story.

Original layout: Broad caption feet with short accent end rules; side by side in landscape, stacked in portrait.

Inputs: title (40), caption1 (24), caption2 (24), picture1 (image), picture2 (image). All pictures are replaceable local image variables; samples are original geometric coastal studies drawn offline with Pillow. No external sources.

Theme: core default colours, token fonts, weight, radius and pace. Caption and catalogue text sits on opaque panels with border-box sizing and font-ready fitting retaining full copy in titles. Both aspects respect title safety and portrait controls.

Motion: whole-layer presets with a 0.25 s exit; zero frame captures and budget 0.
