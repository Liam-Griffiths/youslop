# Zoom detail

Purpose: A picture and a magnified region controlled by focal coordinates.

Original layout: Accent focus cross on a contain-fit original and a separate accent-ringed, twice-magnified inset; focal fractions refer to original image coordinates.

Inputs: title (40), caption (32), focal_x (number), focal_y (number), picture (image). All pictures are replaceable local image variables; samples are original geometric coastal studies drawn offline with Pillow. No external sources.

Theme: core default colours, token fonts, weight, radius and pace. Caption and catalogue text sits on opaque panels with border-box sizing and font-ready fitting retaining full copy in titles. Both aspects respect title safety and portrait controls.

Motion: whole-layer presets with a 0.25 s exit; zero frame captures and budget 0.
