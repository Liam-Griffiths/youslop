# Logo sting

Purpose: Introduce a channel using its local logo, wordmark and short strapline.

Variables: title (text, 24 characters), tagline (text, 40 characters) Optional local logo: original geometric Pillow mark; empty image leaves a wordmark.

Motion: A local logo scales in, followed by a rising wordmark and strapline. Final 0.25 s fade. Capture budget: 0; no frame capture.

Layout: 16:9 and 9:16, token colours/fonts/weight/radius/pace; wrap maximum copy without truncation. Lower thirds use opaque panels, support all four corners on transparency, and clear portrait controls. Part/title and channel cards stay within title safety. No external references or platform styling.

Review: maximum W copy, all overlay corners, reverse seeks, default and alternate theme; generate local posters and previews.
