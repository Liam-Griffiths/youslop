# T20.13 Visuals list and sources, Channels index cards
Phase 20 · Size S · Depends on: — · Findings: UX-24, UX-26
*Written 2026-11-11 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the T20.1 review.*

## Goal
The Visuals list rows are compact, the source tabs fit, and the first tab
is a useful one. The Channels index shows each channel as a card.

## Why
- **Kind chips.** They sit detached under each Visuals list row, doubling
  its height.
- **Source tabs.** They wrap, leaving "Upload" alone on a second row.
- **Default tab.** Footage is the default even when the project has no
  footage of its own.
- **Mix bar colour.** Block is green there, the "done" colour.
- **Channels index.** It's a bare list of links (`j2-13-visuals.png`,
  `j4-01-channels.png`).

## Read first
- `static/js/tabs/media.js` (or `visuals.js`): the list rows, the source
  tabs and the mix bar;
- `docs/design/studio-flow.md` §8 (kinds and colours);
- `static/js/tabs/channels.js` (the index).

## Steps
1. **Kind chip.** Move it into the row, at the right of the meta line, as a
   guide-style chip.
2. **Source tabs.** One row: they scroll horizontally if needed, and
   "Upload" becomes an icon-and-label button at the end.
3. **Default source.**
   - Footage only when the project links a shoot or a footage clip is in
     use.
   - Otherwise Archive.
   - Library when a linked folder exists and the format prefers it.
4. **Mix bar colours.** Blocks and scenes use `--cool`, per the guide's
   kind colours. Keep the text legend.
5. **Channel cards.** Each card shows the name, the theme swatch (accent
   pair on the ground), the video count, the last updated date and "Open".
   The empty state reads "No channels yet. A channel carries a look and
   defaults across videos." with "New channel".

## Acceptance
- At 1280×720 the source tabs are one row.
- Visuals rows are one card tall.
- The Channels index shows cards.
