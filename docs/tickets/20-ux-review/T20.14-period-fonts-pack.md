# T20.14 A period faces pack (mid-century and other eras)
Phase 20 · Size S · Depends on: — · Findings: guide question G4 · **Owner decision needed before starting**
*Written 2026-11-11 (Claude Opus 5.5). Owner, 2026-11-11: "For the test channel let's make it about great hotels of the past. Give it a 50s mid century aesthetics. Is that possible even."*

## Goal
A built-in content pack of a few OFL display faces, so a channel can look
like a period without the user finding fonts. The first faces are
mid-century: a 1950s brush or sign script and a geometric sans.

## Why
In T20.1 the "Grand Hotels" channel took a mid-century palette well:
- cream `#F3E5C8`, walnut `#3B2A1E`, turquoise `#2A9D8F`, mustard
  `#E3A33B`;
- 14 px corners.

But block fonts are limited to Inter, Archivo Narrow, EB Garamond and
JetBrains Mono, plus pack fonts. That's not enough for a 1950s hotel sign.

## Read first
- `app/block_theme.py` `fonts()` (pack fonts are already supported as
  `pack:<ref>`);
- `app/packs.py` and the Starter pack layout;
- `resources/fonts/README.md` (licences).

## Steps
1. **Choose faces.** Pick 3–4 OFL faces from Google Fonts. Candidates:
   - a mid-century sign script such as "Pacifico" or "Yellowtail";
   - a geometric sans such as "Josefin Sans" or "Poppins";
   - optionally an Art Deco face such as "Poiret One" for the 1920s and
     1930s;
   - optionally a 1970s rounded face.

   **Ask the owner to approve the list** before downloading. Downloads
   happen once, by hand, and are committed with their OFL texts.
2. **Ship them.** Add them as a built-in pack ("Period faces") so they show
   in the Block theme font selects, with sizes and weights checked by
   `block check` text-fit.
3. **A preset.** Add a "Mid-century" block-theme preset to the pack: the
   palette above with the turquoise darkened to pass 3:1 on cream (about
   `#1F7A70`), the script for titles and the sans for body.

## Acceptance
- On a channel, choosing the Mid-century preset renders the Blocks grid in
  that look with no contrast warning.
- Each font has its OFL text in the pack.
