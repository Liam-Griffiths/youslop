# T20.6 Project bar at small sizes, one accent on Review & publish
Phase 20 · Size S · Depends on: — · Findings: UX-04, UX-05, UX-22
*Written 2026-11-11 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the T20.1 review.*

## Goal
The project bar stays on one row at 1280×720, and every stage has exactly
one accent button.

## Why
- **Voice at 1280×720:** the project bar breaks. The title floats, the extra
  buttons crowd it, and "Next: Visuals" drops onto the stage bar
  (`s-04-voice.png`).
- **Duplicate controls:** Voice shows "Microphone setup" and "Draft voice
  settings" in the bar, and again in the page.
- **Accents:** Review & publish shows three accent buttons at once: "Next:
  Review by Claude", "Render the video" and "download" (`j2-15-publish.png`).

## Read first
- `static/js/shell.js` (the project bar);
- `static/js/tabs/voice.js`;
- `static/js/tabs/publish.js` (or wherever the finish list renders);
- `docs/design/studio-flow.md` §4 and the "one accent" rule.

## Steps
1. **Collapse the bar.** Below 1400 px, the project bar's secondary buttons
   (beyond Undo and Redo) move into More. The title truncates with an
   ellipsis and a tooltip, never wrapping.
2. **One copy of each Voice control.** Keep the in-page controls and drop
   the bar copies.
3. **Review & publish.** Only the finish list's next action is accent.
   "Render the video" and "Download" become secondary or light buttons.
   Change "download" to "Download".
4. Add a GJS or snapshot assertion: each stage route renders at most one
   visible `.btn.primary`.

## Acceptance
- At 1280×720 every stage's project bar is one row.
- The one-accent assertion passes on all six stages of the example.
