# T20.5 Plain errors with a next step, and quieter jobs
Phase 20 · Size M · Depends on: — · Findings: UX-06, UX-18
*Written 2026-11-11 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the T20.1 review.*

## Goal
Every error the user sees is one plain sentence plus an action. Raw
exception text goes behind a "Details" fold. Background jobs stay in the
jobs strip, in plain words, without internal ids.

## Why
The review saw these raw errors:
- "RuntimeError: …" with "FAILED: …" when the writing model failed;
- "Microphone blocked: Permission denied";
- "FFError: ffmpeg failed: [in#0 @ 0x…]";
- "StageError: TypeError: window.__seek is not a function".

None of them says what to do. Separately, the "timeline sound" job pops a
card at the top of Review & publish and Script reading "Measuring the audio
for linear master normalization", and the drawer shows ids like
`j0f31af6862`. Screenshots in `docs/ux-review/2026-11-11/`: j2-03, j2-20,
j8-03, j8-04, j2-15.

## Read first
- `static/js/core.js` (`toast`, `api`, `watchJob`);
- `app/jobs.py` (the error text on a failed job);
- `app/llm.py` (error types);
- `static/js/tabs/record.js` (`ensureMic`, microphone errors); `static/js/jobs.js`
  and `static/js/shell.js` (drawer and strip); `static/js/timeline/playback.js`
  (background sound/proxy failures), `static/js/timeline/agent.js` (inline errors).

## Steps
1. **Map errors.** Add a small `friendlyError(err, context)` mapping in
   `static/js/core.js`:
   - model unavailable or failed: "The writing model didn't answer. Check
     Settings → Writing model, then Retry";
   - microphone `NotAllowedError`: "The microphone is blocked. Allow it in
     your system settings, or use a draft voice for now";
   - `NotFoundError`: "No microphone found";
   - ffmpeg input missing: "A file this needs is missing: <relative path>";
   - anything else: "That didn't work: <first sentence>".

   Toasts and job cards show the friendly line plus "Details" (the raw
   text, selectable).
2. **Model failures on the server.** In `jobs.py`, record a short `reason`
   field, such as model, network, disk or missing file, as well as the
   message, so the UI can choose the action. Old jobs without it still
   display.
3. **Job cards.**
   - Show them only on the stage that started the job, or on the project
     page for project jobs that change what you see. Background mixes and
     proxies (`writes=False` jobs) show only in the strip.
   - Rename the user-facing status lines in the timeline sound job ("Mixing
     the sound").
   - Drop job ids from the drawer.
   - Make the card's "dismiss" read "Dismiss".
4. Add tests for the mapping and for the job `reason` field.

## Acceptance
