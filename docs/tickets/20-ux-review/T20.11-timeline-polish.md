# T20.11 Timeline polish from the Phase 17 reviews
Phase 20 · Size M · Depends on: T20.4 · Findings: UX-20, UX-30
*Written 2026-11-11 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the T17.4–T17.6 reviews and T20.1.*

## Goal
The Phase 17 gestures feel settled. Nothing jumps, nothing covers the
playhead, and small items can be grabbed.

## Why
Each of these was logged during review:
- Selecting a section grows the ruler row by 22 px, so every lane jumps.
- The section toolbar can sit over the playhead handle, and the drop hint
  overlaps the toolbar text.
- The always-visible nearest "+" catches drags that start on it.
- A 4 s overlay at fit zoom is 25 px wide, and its two 8 px resize handles
  leave little room to move it.
- The graphic mini-toolbar sits over the voice lane.
- A new title card insert shows a blank tile on Pictures.
- Undo and Redo stay enabled when there's nothing to undo.

Screenshots: `t-section-toolbar.png`, `t-graphic-toolbar.png`.

## Read first
- `static/js/timeline/sections.js`, `inserts.js`, `trims.js`,
  `graphic_gestures.js`, `draw.js`;
- `static/js/shell.js` (Undo and Redo);
- `app/undo.py` (index state).

## Steps
1. **Reserve the toolbar row.** The ruler row always has room for the
   section toolbar, so selecting a section never moves the lanes. The
   toolbar and hints position themselves to avoid the playhead handle's
   x-range, flipping to the other side of the band.
2. **"+" buttons.** Hide them while any `TL.drag` is active. A press on "+"
   that moves more than 3 px passes through as a scrub.
3. **Handles on narrow items.** Below 40 px wide, resize handles shrink to
   4 px and the middle stays a move target. Below 16 px wide, only a move is
   possible, and resizing goes through the inspector or `[`/`]`.
4. **Graphic toolbar.** Sits above the Graphics lane in the reserved row,
   not over Voice.
5. **Card tiles.** Title card inserts draw their text on the Pictures tile
   (cheap DOM text, not a render).
6. **Undo and Redo state.** Disable the buttons when the undo index says
   there's nothing to undo or redo, with a tooltip naming the next step
   ("Undo: Moved 3 paragraphs").
7. Add GJS tests for steps 1–3 and 6.

## Acceptance
- Selecting a section changes no lane's `getBoundingClientRect().top`.
- A 25 px overlay can be moved by dragging its middle.
- Undo is disabled on a fresh project.
