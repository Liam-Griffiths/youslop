## Steps
1. Resolve insert pictures locally from metadata, without copying originals:
   clip uses the shoot's `source_id` and range (not a required `clip_id`);
   picture uses Library `asset_id`; block uses the existing segment-block path
   with validated vars; card uses `titles.render_chapter` and project styling.
   Reuse the existing picture/overlay/chapter paths, including preview hides.
   Replace the temporary `_render_segment` and `_render` guards.
2. Replace `_concat_takes`' temporary guard. Inserts always occupy their exact
   explicit seconds, including when no paragraphs have takes. Silence is the
   default; `clip.source_audio` selects only its source range. Source audio is
   never stored as a synthetic narration take. Keep the music bed continuous.
   Include source/range/audio choice and source file changes in mix/proxy keys
   and readiness. Captions and spoken-word checks skip inserts.
3. Make source clip picture/audio agree on the range despite leading/trailing
   half-transitions, HEAD_PAD and TAIL_PAD. Check terminal inserts, consecutive
   inserts, intro-only projects, missing originals/proxies, and sponsor reads.
   An insert may open a chapter; its chapter card must have a real visible
   hold without silently lengthening the insert or shifting narration.
4. Deferred to [T17.2c](T17.2c-inserts-script.md) to keep the render/audio change
   near the diff limit: preserve inserts during whole-script replacement and
   exclude them from narration planning, TTS, media selection and word checks.
   Keep the replacement guards until preservation is tested.
5. Retain legacy project/storyboard/golden/key bytes. The `insert` field is
   omitted when empty, including the two `dataclasses.asdict` key paths.
   Extend the existing isolated tests for real audio filtergraph timing,
   source-audio on/off, card/block dispatch and render/cache behavior.
6. At most one quarter-size insert section preview, on synthetic temp data.
   No full renders, 3D suite, benchmarks, network calls or real LLMs.
