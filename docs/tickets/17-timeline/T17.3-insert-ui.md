# T17.3 Insert from the timeline
Phase 17 · Size M · Depends on: T17.2

## Goal
From the Edit stage you can put a clip, block, picture or card between any two
items on the main track, and watch the rest move to make room.

## Read first
- T17.2 and its Implementer notes. `static/js/timeline/draw.js`, `layers.js`,
  `sponsors.js` (`tlSponsorAdd`, `tlWireSponsorDrag`: the existing insert-and-drag
  pattern), `graphics.js` `openGraphicPicker`, `blocks_browser.js` `renderBlocks`.
- `docs/design/studio-flow.md`.

## Steps
1. A small `+` at every edit point on the pictures lane (on hover, and always
   at the playhead's nearest edit point). Clicking it opens an insert menu:
   Clip from footage (T14.3 named clips and shoot sources), Block, Picture
   from Library, Title card.
2. Smart insert: a key (`.` or `Insert`) inserts at the edit point nearest the
   playhead, as in Resolve's cut page.
3. Drag from a side panel: a collapsible "Add" panel beside the inspector lists
   recent clips, blocks for this aspect and Library pictures. Dragging over the
   pictures lane shows a drop marker at the nearest edit point and a ghost of
   the ripple. Drop creates the insert.
4. The new insert is selected, and the inspector shows its fields (length,
   source in/out, block vars, audio on/off).
5. Delete or Backspace on a selected insert removes it, with undo (T17.1).

## Acceptance
- Insert a clip after the intro, by `+` and by drag. Later items move by its
  length; titles and overlays stay with their paragraphs.
- Keyboard-only path works: select an edit point, `.`, choose, Enter.
