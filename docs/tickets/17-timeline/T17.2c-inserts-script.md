## Steps
1. Test and implement preservation of insert ids/metadata/order during whole
   script generation/import. Define how a replaced or removed paragraph anchor
   maps onto the new script, including intro, consecutive and terminal inserts
   and a replacement with fewer paragraphs. Keep the single Project.slides list.
2. Keep recorded takes/history reachable through replacement and persistence;
   never overwrite/delete recordings. Preserve existing paragraph-edit behavior

3. Exclude inserts from narration planning, rewriting, consistency and research
   verification, TTS, automatic media selection and word-based findings. Test

4. Adjust stages/counts where needed: inserts are pictures, not unwritten or
   unread paragraphs. Preserve legacy project/storyboard/key bytes.
5. Remove whole-script replacement guards only once preservation tests pass.

## Implementer notes
T17.2b implements all four render kinds, exact insert/source audio assembly,
chapter holds and cache invalidation. At pickup, generation/import rejected
inserts with guards naming T17.2c. This ticket removes those guards after
testing preservation. Individual insert rewrites remain rejected. Prepared
insert rows have no narration and locked_media=True, but
entry points must explicitly exclude inserts rather than rely on those values.
The one quarter-size synthetic insert preview was already produced in T17.2b;
its Log records the path for reviewer listening/visual judgement.

Replacement policy (2026-11-11): `inserts.replace_script` preserves each
insert row and its fields in the single slides list. Its boundary is the number
of preceding spoken paragraphs, clamped to the replacement's length. Opening
inserts remain first; inserts after the old final paragraph follow the new final
paragraph even when the script grows. Collapsed boundaries retain original
order. An insert-only project's rows remain before newly written narration.
Removed paragraph ids are replaced by new ids; their takes/history are retired
with owner ids through `undo.retain_recordings`, including without undo tracking.
