# T17.6 Blocks and graphics on the timeline
Phase 17 · Size M · Depends on: T17.3

## Goal
Add, move, copy and remove overlay blocks directly on the timeline, as fast as
in a consumer editor.

## Read first
- `static/js/timeline/graphics.js` (`openGraphicPicker`, `tlGraphicAdd`),
  `inspect.js` `tlGraphicInspector`, `app/block_overlays.py` `validate`,
  `app/overlay_plan.py`, and the T17.3 Add panel.

## Steps
1. Blocks in the T17.3 Add panel: drag an overlay block onto the graphics
   lane at a time (it attaches to the paragraph under that time, at that
   paragraph-relative second), or onto the main track as a full-screen
   block insert (T17.2).
2. Moving an overlay past its paragraph's end reattaches it to the next
   paragraph, instead of stopping at the boundary.
3. Ctrl+D duplicates the selection just after itself. Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V copy
   and paste at the playhead. Delete removes it. Arrow keys nudge one frame,
   Shift+arrow ten frames. All of these support undo.
4. A floating mini-toolbar over the selected item: Split (razor), Duplicate,
   Delete, and Replace block. Replacing keeps timing and compatible vars.
5. Overlay length handles on both edges, snapping like T17.5.

## Acceptance
- Drag the lower-third block from the panel onto paragraph 2 at 1.5 s:
  the overlay is saved with `start` 1.5 on paragraph 2 and previews in place.
- Duplicate, paste and nudge all work and undo cleanly.
