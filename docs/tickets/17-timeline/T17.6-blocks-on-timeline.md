# T17.6 Blocks and graphics on the timeline
Phase 17 · Size M · Depends on: T17.3

## Goal
Add, move, copy and remove overlay blocks directly on the timeline, as fast as
in a consumer editor.

## Read first
- `static/js/timeline/graphics.js` (`openGraphicPicker`, `tlGraphicAdd`),
  `inspect.js` `tlGraphicInspector`, `app/block_overlays.py` `validate`,
  `app/overlay_plan.py`, and the T17.3 Add panel.

## Steps
1. Blocks in the T17.3 Add panel: drag an overlay block onto the graphics
   lane at a time (it attaches to the paragraph under that time, at that
   paragraph-relative second), or onto the main track as a full-screen
   block insert (T17.2).
2. Moving an overlay past its paragraph's end reattaches it to the next
   paragraph, instead of stopping at the boundary.
3. Ctrl+D duplicates the selection just after itself. Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V copy
   and paste at the playhead. Delete removes it. Arrow keys nudge one frame,
   Shift+arrow ten frames. All of these support undo.
4. A floating mini-toolbar over the selected item: Split (razor), Duplicate,
   Delete, and Replace block. Replacing keeps timing and compatible vars.
5. Overlay length handles on both edges, snapping like T17.5.

## Acceptance
- Drag the lower-third block from the panel onto paragraph 2 at 1.5 s:
  the overlay is saved with `at` 1.5 on paragraph 2 and previews in place.
- Duplicate, paste and nudge all work and undo cleanly.

## Implementer notes

- T17.3 already supplies block drag payloads and full-screen inserts. Extend its
  Add panel and keep `renderBlocks` as the only picker.
- Graphics use stored `at` (paragraph-relative seconds), not `start`.
  Render-time resolution clips duration at
  paragraph boundaries; moving keeps stored duration and clears word anchoring.
- Use `slides-batch` for atomic reattachment and multi-graphic actions. Section
  shortcuts win when a section is selected; otherwise graphic C/V/D win over a
  retained section clipboard. Keep T17.7's shared clamp for group movement.
- Reuse `tlTrackHint` for move/length feedback. The graphic toolbar belongs
  above Graphics, below the ruler; preserve ruler/playhead/body scrubbing.
- Snapshot harness and visual/playback judgement are reviewer-run. Implementation
  verification uses the unit suite and GJS gesture tests without a browser.

Chromium probe on the example: the mini-toolbar shows Split, Duplicate, Delete and Replace block; nudge moves one frame; Ctrl+C then Ctrl+V at 1:40 pastes onto paragraph 10 at 1.40 s; undo removes it; no console errors. One bug:

The same lookup feeds delete, split, replace and move/reattach, and `tlGraphicBatch` re-selects with `ids.includes(s.id)`. Key every graphic lookup and re-selection by `(sid, id)`: `tlGraphicLive`, the `ids` list in `tlGraphicBatch` (push `{sid,id}` pairs, and use the target sid for pasted/duplicated/reattached items), and anything in `graphic_gestures.js`, `graphics.js` or `ergonomics.js` this ticket touched that matches by id. Keep new ids globally unique (`graphic-<uuid>`, as now). Add a GJS test with two paragraphs whose overlays share an id: duplicate, delete, split and move each act on the selected paragraph's overlay only.
