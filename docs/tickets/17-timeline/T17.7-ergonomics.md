# T17.7 Timeline ergonomics
Phase 17 · Size M · Depends on: —

## Goal
The timeline is comfortable at any project length: it opens fitted, zooms
around the pointer, has readable lanes, and lets you select several items.

## Why
The Edit snapshot of an 11 s project draws the whole timeline in about 140 px
of a 1600 px window, so items are a few pixels wide and labels are cut off.

## Read first
- `static/js/timeline/core.js` (`tlMount`, zoom slider), `draw.js`, `layers.js`,
  `playback.js` (`tlWireScrub`), `edit.js`, and `docs/design/studio-flow.md`.
  Note the T14.2c follow-up: never read `TL.data` before it loads.

## Steps
1. Fit on open: the initial zoom fits the whole project to the track width
   (with a minimum zoom). A "Fit" button and `Shift+Z` refit. Remember the
   zoom per project in localStorage (try/catch).
2. Ctrl or Cmd plus the wheel zooms around the pointer, and the plain wheel
   scrolls horizontally when the track overflows. `+` and `-` zoom around the
   playhead. While playing, the view scrolls to keep the playhead visible.
3. Lanes: taller pictures lane with a thumbnail strip, item labels that stay
   readable (truncate with a title tooltip), lane headers that stay visible
   while scrolling horizontally, and compact lanes collapsible per row.
4. Multi-select: Shift-click adds to the selection; drag on empty track
   space draws a marquee. A multi-selection moves together on drag (same
   snapping) and deletes together, for the existing editable item kinds
   listed in Implementer notes. The inspector shows "3 items" with the
   shared fields only.
5. A hover time readout and the item name under the pointer. The shortcuts
   overlay (`?`) lists every new key.
6. Optional: a voice waveform on the voice lane from existing take levels.
   wavesurfer.js v7 is allowed if vendored with its licence; drawing the
   levels on a canvas is fine too.

## Acceptance
- An 11 s project and a 20 min project both open fitted and readable at 1280
  and 1600 px wide.
- Wheel zoom keeps the time under the pointer fixed.
- Marquee-selecting three titles and dragging them moves all three, and the
  move undoes as one edit through the normal save path (T17.1 is committed).

## Implementer notes
- T17.1 is committed. Group edits use one validated batch PATCH and one
  `store.save`, with `editLabel` / `editGesture` supplied to `api()`.
- Multi-select covers visible timeline items; group moves apply to the existing
  movable title, cue and graphic items. Pictures/paragraph reordering belongs
  to T17.4. Delete supports titles, cues, chapters and graphics; source audio
  is never touched. A drawn cut requires a cue: deleting its last cue is
  rejected, because an empty cue list regenerates defaults in `cuts.cues_for`.
  Mixed selections expose only actions shared by every item.
- Fit needs a zoom below the old 2 px/s floor for long projects, and above the
  old 60 px/s ceiling for short ones. Restore saved zoom only after data loads.
- Snapshot/browser and visual review are reviewer-run, as specified in Verify.

### Found in review (2026-11-11, Claude Opus 5.5)
Chromium check on the Tube Mail example at 1600 px:
- fit-on-open works (8:48 fills the track) and the thumbnails strip shows;
- shift-select of 3 graphics gives "3 items" with Move together and Delete;
- moving by -0.5 s moves all three.

Two problems:
1. **Undo is refused on the Edit stage (a T17.1 regression, fix it here).**
   - `_restore_project_edit` returns 409 whenever `jobs.active_for(pid)` is non-empty.
   - Opening Edit starts "timeline sound" (`timeline_mix`) and "timeline proxy" jobs, so on Edit undo fails with "Wait for this project's running jobs to finish" while they run. That's several seconds on every open and after every edit that triggers a proxy, and minutes during a render or segment preview.
   - Fix: give `jobs.start` a `writes=True` keyword (default True, for safety), and set `writes=False` on jobs that never save the project:
     - timeline sound;
     - segment, sponsor, intro, end-screen and other `* preview` jobs, after checking each one's `work` for `store.save`;
     - local speed test.
   - `jobs.active_for(pid, writing=True)` filters on it, and undo/redo refuse only for writing jobs.
   - Timeline proxy saves after downloading pictures, but it reloads under the lock first. Mark it `writes=False` only if its save is just picture downloads done that way, and say which you chose in the Log.
   - Add a unit test: undo succeeds while a `writes=False` job is running, and is refused while a writing job runs.
   - Note the fix in T17.1's Log too.
2. **"Move together" silently does nothing when the shared clamp is 0.**
   - Example: the third graphic already ends at its paragraph's end, so +0.5 s clamps to 0 and nothing happens and nothing is said.
   - When the clamped delta differs from the request, move by the clamped amount and show a short `tlMsg` or toast: "Moved by 0.20 s; ‘<item>’ reaches the end of its paragraph." When it's 0, say "Can't move further: ‘<item>’ is at the end of its paragraph." (or the start).
   - Group drag gets the same treatment. Its existing snapping can stay.
