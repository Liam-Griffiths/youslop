# T17.4 Select sections and move everything in them
Phase 17 · Size L · Depends on: T17.2, T17.1

## Goal
Select a section of the video (one paragraph, several, or a stretch of time)
and move, cut, paste, duplicate or delete it with everything in it: the
pictures, inserts, narration, titles, overlay blocks, cues and chapter starts.
The rest of the timeline closes up behind it and opens up where it lands.

## Why
The owner, 2026-11-09: "I want the edit moving etc to be easier. Selecting
sections, everything, and moving them." Today each item moves on its own lane,
and the main track can't move at all.

## Read first
- `static/js/timeline/edit.js` (T4.5 drag pattern, `tlSnap`), `inspect.js`
  (`TL.selection`, `tlSelect`), `playback.js` (`tlWireScrub`, the ruler),
  `sponsors.js` drag, `app/edits.py`, the slide edit routes in `app/main.py`,
  and `Project.picture_runs` / `schema.group_slides` (`picture_every`).

## Steps
1. **Section selection**, as a range on the main track snapped to edit
   points, highlighted across every lane so it's obvious everything inside
   is included. Ways to make one:
   - drag along the ruler, or along the pictures lane with Shift;
   - click a paragraph, then Shift+click another to extend;
   - set I and O marks, then press X;
   - Ctrl+A selects the whole main track.
   A plain click still selects a single item, as now.
2. **Move by dragging** the selected section (past a 6 px threshold, so a
   click still selects):
   - a ghost of the whole section follows the pointer;
   - the gap closes behind it, and the drop point opens at edit points only.
   Escape cancels. Alt+Left and Alt+Right move the section one place, with no
   pointer needed.
3. **Section commands**, all on a mini-toolbar over the selection and all
   undoable as one edit each:
   - Cut, Copy and Paste: Ctrl+X, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V. Paste inserts at the edit
     point nearest the playhead and ripples.
   - Duplicate: Ctrl+D places a copy right after.
   - Delete with ripple: Delete or Backspace.
4. **Rules:**
   - Moving narrated paragraphs reorders the script; the drop hint says so.
   - Takes travel with their paragraphs and are never re-cut.
   - A duplicated narrated paragraph points at the same take file without
     copying audio, and is marked as a duplicate in the script view.
   - Grouped pictures (`picture_every` > 1) move as a group unless the
     section boundary splits the group. Then the split paragraphs start their
     own group, with a hint.
   - Sponsor slots inside a section move with it.
   - Chapter starts move with their paragraph.
5. **API:** `POST /api/projects/{pid}/sections` with
   `{op: move|delete, ids, to, version}` for this part (`copy` in T17.4b).
   `ids` is a contiguous range of main-track row ids, including inserts;
   `to` is the row to insert before, or `""` for the end, and `version` is
   the project's `updated_at` from selection time.
   - Validates under the lock: a permutation for a move, and a 409 if the
     project changed.
   - Applies atomically, with an undo label ("Moved 3 paragraphs").

## Acceptance
- Selecting paragraphs 2–4 and dragging them before paragraph 1 changes the
  script order. The timeline, storyboard and check all agree, and every
  attached item keeps its paragraph-relative time.
- Copying a section and pasting it after the intro duplicates pictures,
  inserts and graphics, and later items move by the section's length.
- Delete with ripple leaves no gap.
- Each operation undoes as one step and restores the project exactly.
- No take or media file is created, removed or rewritten.

### Found in review (2026-11-11, Claude Opus 5.5)
Reviewed: unit suite 2333 passed; Chromium probe on the example (section highlight, real drag of paragraphs 2–4 to the front, undo restores the order exactly, Ctrl-click toggles in and out, no console errors). The cue-lead fix in `kit.place` is accepted: under the old clock, a cue dragged on the first paragraph of a group with overlap or head pad was stored `lead` earlier than where it was dropped. Fix these three:

1. **Pre and end sponsor reads get pinned to a paragraph.** `sections.apply` converts every enabled sponsor slot to `placement='mid'` with an `after_slide_id`, including `pre` (after the hook) and `end` slots. After any section edit, moving the last paragraph earlier drags the end read into the middle of the video, and the pre read follows whichever paragraph it happened to land after. Only convert time-based `mid` slots (`at_s`, no `after_slide_id`); leave `pre` and `end` slots untouched, and leave slots that already have `after_slide_id` alone. Add a test: an end slot and a pre slot stay `end`/`pre` after moving the last paragraph to the front, and the rendered placements still put them at the end and after the hook.
2. **Drag ghost shows lane-label fragments.** The ghost clones whole `.tl-lane` rows, including `.tl-layer-label`, so clipped label text ("HICS", "SORS", "ONS", "TERS") shows at the ghost's left edge. Clone only the lane contents, without labels.
3. **The section toolbar covers the pictures lane.** It sits over the first thumbnails of the section and hides them. Place it in the ruler row above the band (or just above the track), clamped inside the visible scroll area, so no lane content is covered.
