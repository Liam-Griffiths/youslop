# T14.3 Clip finder: transcript view, select by words, suggested moments
Phase 14 · Size M · Depends on: T14.1, T11.5
*Written 2026-11-08 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the owner's clipping request.*

## Goal
A Clips screen for any imported recording, with two ways to work:
- **Transcript mode** (after a whole transcription): read and search the transcript beside the player, select words to make a clip, and see suggested moments.
- **Manual mode** (no transcription needed): scrub the player and waveform, and mark in and out by time. Optionally, "Transcribe this clip" transcribes just that range, for captions and word-accurate trims.

Each clip is a named time range on the original.

## Why
Finding the 40 seconds worth posting in a two-hour stream is the slow part of clipping. Selecting by words is faster and more exact than scrubbing.

- T14.1's Log (transcript shape), `static/js/tabs/footage.js` (logger player and keys), `app/footage.py` (Clip, `edit_clips`), `docs/design/studio-flow.md`.

## Steps
1. **Route and modes.** `#/footage/<shoot>/clips/<source>`. The page opens in the mode that fits:
   - **Not transcribed:** manual mode, with a banner offering "Transcribe the whole recording (about N min on this machine)". Transcribing is never required.
   - **Transcribed:** transcript mode. A toggle switches back to manual mode at any time.
   - **Transcript mode:** the transcript on the left (virtualised for hours of text), the proxy player (or waveform) on the right, search, and a timeline strip below.
   - **Manual mode:** the player, a full-width waveform and the timeline strip, with keyboard scrubbing (J/K/L, arrows by 1 s, Shift+arrows by 10 s). Partly transcribed ranges show their words inline on the strip.
   - Either mode lists the clips made so far.
2. **Select to clip.** In transcript mode, drag across words; in either mode, press `I`/`O` at the playhead or drag on the waveform.
   - **Manual mode without words:** ends snap to the nearest pause of at least 0.25 s on the audio envelope (`words._runs`-style voiced runs). Each clip gets "Transcribe this clip" (range mode, T14.1), which then refines the ends to word boundaries.
   - **Selecting words:** Ends snap to word boundaries, then outwards to the nearest pause of at least 0.25 s, with up to 0.4 s of padding. The clip shows its length against targets (15/30/60/90 s). Clips are stored as `{id, source_id, in_s, out_s, title, hook, note}` in the shoot, reusing T11.5's Clip fields where they fit.
3. **Suggested moments (offline; needs a whole transcript).** Score sentence windows of 20–90 s:
   - a strong opening sentence (a question, a number or a claim word);
   - higher speech energy than the recording's median;
   - few long pauses;
   - a complete ending.

   Show the top 10 as dismissable suggestions, each with its reason ("opens with a question · 42 s").
4. **Suggested moments (LLM, opt-in).** "Rank with Claude" sends sentence windows only (text, never audio) and shows the token estimate first. It returns up to 10 moments, each with a hook line. In tests, `llm.complete_json` is mocked.
5. **Make clip projects (handoff after T14.4 exists; tracked in T14.3b).** "Make clips" turns selected clips into projects (T14.4), one per clip, linked back to the source and range. A clip without words can still become a project. Captions (T14.5) then offer "Transcribe this clip" first; a clip without captions never needs transcription.

## Acceptance
- Manual mode with no transcript: I/O at the playhead makes a clip that snaps to envelope pauses. "Transcribe this clip" (mocked engine) calls the engine for that range only, and then the ends refine to word boundaries.
- On a fixture transcript, selecting words from "So the" to "changed everything." makes a clip that snaps to the pause boundaries. Search jumps to matches.
- The offline suggestions return windows within 20–90 s that end at sentence ends (unit tested).
- Headless Chromium: 0 console errors. Keyboard I/O, Space and J/K/L work in the clip finder.
