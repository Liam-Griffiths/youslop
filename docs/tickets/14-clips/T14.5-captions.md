# T14.5 Captions: burned-in styles from word timings, plus SRT/VTT
Phase 14 · Size M · Depends on: T14.1, T14.4
*Written 2026-11-08 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the owner's clipping request.*

## Goal
Optional burned-in captions from word timings, in a few styles: phrase, word-by-word highlight, and one big word at a time. The block theme drives them. Every project can also export SRT and VTT sidecars, long videos included.

## Why
Owner: "speech to text for optional subtitles." Most short-form clips are watched muted. The kinetic caption blocks (`kinetic_caption_*`) and the essay caption engine already draw words in time. This ticket feeds them transcript words and makes them a per-project option.

## Read first
- `static/blocks/kinetic_caption_*`, `app/render.py` (essay phrase captions, `_essay_phrases`), `app/block_overlays.py`, `app/words.py`, T14.1's Log.

## Steps
0. **No words yet.** Turning captions on for a clip whose range isn't fully covered by the transcript offers "Transcribe this clip" (T14.1 range mode, seconds) instead of failing.
1. **Caption plan.** `app/captions.py` turns word timings into phrases: at most 2 lines and about 32 characters a line on 9:16, breaking at punctuation and pauses, at most 3 s a phrase, and no phrase across a cut. Inputs are transcript words (`stt`), TTS words, or estimated words (`est`); with `est` timings a P2 finding notes the low confidence.
1b. **Timing quality (added 2026-11-08, owner: "speech to text would need timings for subtitles").**
   - **Clean word ends first.** Whisper-family engines often stretch a word's end across the pause after it. Before phrasing, clamp each word's end to the next word's start, and cap a single word at about 1.2 s unless it is the last word before a long pause. Keep the raw times in the transcript; clean only in the caption plan.
   - **Know the source of each timing.** Words carry `src`: `stt` (engine word timestamps), `stt-seg` (an engine that only gave sentence times; T14.1 spreads the words evenly), `tts`, or `est`. Phrase captions and SRT/VTT work with any source. Word-highlight and big-word styles need real word times: for `stt-seg` and `est` words they fall back to phrase style for that stretch, with a P2 finding saying why.
   - **Prefer real timings for narration.** When a recorded take has been retimed by speech-to-text (T14.1b's Voice action), use those words instead of the estimates.
   - **Subtitle rules for SRT/VTT.** At least about 0.8 s on screen, at least 2 frames between cues, and no cue spanning a pause longer than about 1.5 s.
2. **Styles.** Phrase (a block caption), word highlight (the current word in the accent colour) and big word (one word, centred, punch-in). Each renders through the overlay path as layers captured once and moved by ffmpeg: one layer per phrase, never per-word ffmpeg nodes, following the kinetic-words rule in `docs/blocks/format.md`. Position is bottom-safe or centre, clear of the Shorts UI zones.
3. **Editing.** In Edit, a captions row on the Timeline shows the phrases. Clicking one edits its text, which updates the transcript override from T14.1. Re-timing comes only from words; there are no free-hand caption timings in this ticket.
4. **Sidecars.** Export SRT and VTT from the same phrase plan for any project, with timings on the final cut's clock (after cuts, sponsor reads and slates). The YouTube upload can attach captions as a caption track (`captions.insert`, mocked in tests).
5. **Checks.** Captions inside the safe areas, a reading speed of at most 20 characters a second, and contrast against the picture (reuse `overlay_checks`).

## Acceptance
- Word-end cleanup: on a fixture where a word's end overlaps a 1 s pause, the cue ends at the next word's start (or the capped end), not after the pause.
- A stretch of `stt-seg` words renders in phrase style even when word highlight is chosen, and a P2 finding names it.
- On a fixture of 40 words with known timings, every phrase satisfies the length and duration rules, and the SRT round-trips (parsed back, the times match within one frame).
- A quarter-size preview of a clip section in each of the three styles, checked by the reviewer.
- A long video's SRT lines up with its sponsor read and slate offsets (unit test on the final-cut clock).
