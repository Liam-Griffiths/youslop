# T14.4 Clip projects: reframe, trim, hook title and graphics
Phase 14 · Size L · Depends on: T14.2, T14.3, T12.10a
*Written 2026-11-08 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the owner's clipping request.*

## Goal
A clip project is a short video whose picture and sound are a range of the original recording. You reframe it for the chosen aspect, trim it on word boundaries, put a hook title over the top and add any graphics (blocks) you like. It uses the existing Edit stage and renders like any project.

## Why
Owner: "clips often have text overlay and a description with a link to the full clip." The text overlay is a block over footage, which T12.10a already supports for paragraphs.

## Read first
- `app/render.py` `_clip_source` (T11.6 footage in/out on the original, proxy for previews), `app/block_overlays.py`, `app/edits.py`, `app/schema.py` `Project`/`Slide`, T14.2's Log (frame size), T14.3's Log (clip store).

## Steps
1. **Model.** `Project.kind = "clip"` with `clip = {shoot_id, source_id, in_s, out_s, aspect, full_url?}`. A clip project has one paragraph per transcript sentence (or one per speaker turn) inside the range. Each paragraph's picture is the original over its range (footage provider, as T11.6), and its narration is the original's audio over the same range: read only, never copied into `takes/`, never treated as a recorded take that can be retired. Captions come from the transcript words (T14.5). The Plan, Script and Voice stages are skipped for clip projects, and the stage bar shows Clip, Edit and Publish.
2. **Trim.** Word-boundary trims at the start and end. Cutting a middle stretch (an "um", a tangent) removes a word range, and the cut is drawn as a jump cut with a 2-frame audio crossfade. Cuts are stored as ranges, and the original is untouched.
3. **Reframe.**
   - For 9:16 and 1:1, a crop box over the 16:9 source, with keyframes at paragraph boundaries. Positions move with a short ease.
   - Presets: centre, left third, right third, and a split "two speakers" layout (top/bottom halves from two crop boxes).
   - The default crop is centre.
   - Face tracking is out of scope (note it in the Log as a follow-up).
4. **Hook title and graphics.** The "Hook" field adds a title overlay block at the top for the first N seconds (or the whole clip). It defaults to the clip's hook text from T14.3, uses the channel's block theme (T12.12), and any overlay block can be used. A progress bar along the bottom is an optional overlay.
5. **Audio.** Loudness-match the clip to the master target. An optional music bed under the speech uses the existing music settings.
6. **Render.** A quarter-size preview of a section, and a full render of a clip when asked. Clips are short, but the full-render rule still applies: an explicit click.

## Acceptance
- A fixture clip project from a 16:9 fixture recording has a 9:16 crop and a hook title. Its quarter-size preview shows the cropped picture moving, the hook title on top, and the audio matching the original range (sample check on the audio).
- The original recording's bytes and mtime are unchanged after making, trimming and rendering a clip.
- No file is added to `takes/`, and nothing in the clip appears as a retirable take.
