# T14.2 Vertical and square output for the whole render pipeline
Phase 14 · Size L · Depends on: —
*Written 2026-11-08 (Claude Opus 5.5). Anchors checked against `dev` at that date.*

## Goal
A project renders at 1080×1920 (9:16) or 1080×1080 (1:1) as well as 1920×1080. Previews, proxies, the Timeline and thumbnails follow its aspect.

## Why
Clips are mostly vertical. Today `config.WIDTH, HEIGHT = 1920, 1080` is fixed, and `Project.aspect` says "rendering stays landscape for now". Blocks already declare 9:16 layouts (T12.3), so the drawn layer is mostly ready. The photographic graph, captions, chapter cards, slates and proxies aren't.

## Read first
- `app/config.py` (`WIDTH`, `HEIGHT`, `preview_size`), `app/render.py` (every `WIDTH`/`HEIGHT` use, `_segment_graph`, `_reframe`, `preview_size()`, `_render`, `_build_proxy`, titles and captions), `app/titles.py`, `app/cuts/kit.py`, `app/board.py`, `app/scenes.py` (`width`/`height` in contexts).
- `app/schema.py` `Project.aspect`.

## Steps
1. Add `Project.aspect` values `portrait` and `square` (`landscape` stays the default) and a single `render.frame_size(project, preview=False)`. Replace fixed `WIDTH`/`HEIGHT` uses in the render path with it. Keep the constants as the landscape defaults.
2. Photographic pictures fill the frame by cover-crop around the chosen focal point (`focal`). Ken Burns moves stay within the crop. The essay layout and captions get portrait and square layouts, or are declared landscape-only with a check finding.
3. Scenes, blocks, cuts and board receive the frame size in context. Where a renderer has no portrait layout, a check finding says so, and it renders letterboxed rather than failing.
4. Proxies, the Timeline player, thumbnails and the stage previews use the project's aspect.
5. Goldens: landscape graphs are byte-identical. Add one portrait and one square graph golden.

## Acceptance
- Every existing landscape golden and cache key is unchanged.
- One quarter-size section preview each at 9:16 and 1:1 of a photo paragraph with a title and captions, checked by the reviewer: nothing is cropped off and text is inside the safe areas.
