Step 3, Step 4 and their Step 5 tests are implemented here. Settings in Step 1,
jitter-safe seams in Step 2, and corresponding remaining tests are tracked in
`T14.1e-settings-and-seams.md`. Original acceptance checks remain binding across
all split tickets; midpoint chunk ownership is still not jitter-safe.

`app/stt/timing.py` measures only bounded mono PCM spans. Segment fallback uses
voiced intervals (`stt-seg`), emits no words on silence, and marks turns after a
pause with measured low energy; these are not speaker identities. Chunk cache
version 2 invalidates prior segment-span output, including replacement-plan reuse.
Voice retiming decodes the processed take to a temporary WAV, then saves only
`take["words"]` under the project lock after reloading and checking the snapshot.
It requires real engine word timestamps (`stt`); silent/segment-only output keeps
the old timings. Text, take history, file references and audio are untouched.
