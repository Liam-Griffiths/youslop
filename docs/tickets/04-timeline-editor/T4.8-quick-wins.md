# T4.8 Quick wins: search, suggest chapters
Phase 4 · Size S · Depends on: T4.1

## Goal
The home list of videos is searchable and filterable, and the Script tab has
a "Suggest chapters" button that proposes chapter marks from section and
beat boundaries.

## Why
Two frequently felt gaps that are cheap after the JS split.

## Read first
- `static/js/tabs/home.js` (after T4.1): the function that renders the
  project list (was `renderHome` or similar in `app.js`; grep `list_projects`
  on the client).
- `app/main.py`: `list_projects` delegates to `app/store.py` (fields returned:
  title, channel_id, updated_at, status); channel names come from `/api/channels`.
- `app/schema.py`: `Slide.section` (`intro|main|conclusion`), `Slide.beat`,
  `Slide.chapter`.
- `app/script_gen.py`: `build_outline` ~76 (what a beat is), `verify_script`.
- `static/js/tabs/script.js`: `paintScript` (was ~2491).

## Steps
1. Home: a search input filtering by title, channel and status as you type
   (client side over the list already fetched); a channel dropdown; sort by
   updated or title. Remember the last query in `localStorage` wrapped in
   try/catch. Empty state text when nothing matches.
2. `script_gen.suggest_chapters(project) -> list[dict]`: pure, no LLM. Walk
   slides; propose a chapter at the first slide of `main`, at each change
   of `beat` after at least 45 s of narration since the last proposal, and
   never within the last 20 s. Text: title-case the `beat` (≤ 6 words), or
   the first 6 words of the narration if `beat` is empty. Returns
   `[{sid, text, t}]`.
3. Route `POST /api/projects/{pid}/chapters/suggest` returns proposals
   without writing; `{apply: true}` writes `slide.chapter` for the listed
   sids (skipping slides that already have one).
4. Script tab button "Suggest chapters" shows the proposals inline with
   checkboxes and an Apply button.
5. Tests: `suggest_chapters` spacing rule and the never-in-last-20-s rule on
   a synthetic project.

## Acceptance
- Typing on the home screen filters immediately; suggestions on the test
  project land on sensible beats and apply.

## Verify
```sh
.venv/bin/pytest tests/unit/test_suggest_chapters.py -q
```

## Don't
- No LLM call for chapter suggestions; they must be instant.

## Implementer notes
*Added 2026-10-26.*

- `suggest_chapters` needs times; take them from `render.timeline(project)`
  paragraph `start`s (estimated for unrecorded paragraphs, which is fine for
  a suggestion). `timeline()` does no rendering.
- Chapter text: `beat` is the outline section heading (2-5 words by the
  outline prompt's own rule), so title-casing it is usually enough.
- Setting `slide.chapter` changes picture grouping (`group_slides` forces a
  new picture at a chapter) and the gap (`GAP_CHAPTER` = 5.2 s × base).
  Say so in the confirmation UI: applying chapters lengthens the video by
  roughly 5 s per chapter and may split picture runs.
- `localStorage` access wrapped in try/catch, as the ticket says; WebKitGTK
  in PyWebView can throw on it.
