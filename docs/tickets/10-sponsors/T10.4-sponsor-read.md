# T10.4 Writing and voicing the read
Phase 10 · Size S · Depends on: T10.2
*Written 2026-10-31 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the owner's brief; anchors checked against `dev` at that date.*

## Goal
From a sponsor brief, Claude drafts short and long reads in the channel's voice; the user edits; the read is voiced (TTS or recorded) as a take with word timings that feed the template.

## Why
A read that sounds like the rest of the video converts better and keeps the viewer.

## Read first
- `app/script_gen.py` prompts; `app/tts.py:speak_to_file`;
  `app/main.py:upload_take`, `_voice_one`; `app/audio_files.py`; T4.9 history;
  `app/schema.py:SponsorSlot.set_take`; `static/js/timeline/sponsors.js`.

## Steps
1. Prompt with brief, channel voice, length target; mock in tests.
2. Voice via the existing paths; takes kept (T4.9).
3. Add draft, voice, upload and history restore controls to the sponsor inspector.
   Show a token estimate before drafting, and apply a draft only on selection.

## Acceptance
- Short and long drafts appear; voicing produces a take with words.
