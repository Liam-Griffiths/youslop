# T10.5 Placement suggestions and checks
Phase 10 · Size S · Depends on: T10.1, T1.5
*Written 2026-10-31 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the owner's brief; anchors checked against `dev` at that date.*

## Goal
The app suggests sponsor positions (pre-roll right after the hook; mid-roll at the chapter boundary nearest 35–50% of runtime; end before the outro) and `check.py` gains sponsor rules.

## Why
Placement decides whether viewers stay; the rules are known and checkable.

## Read first
- `app/check.py` rule pattern; `app/schema.py` FrameSpec and SponsorSlot;
  `app/storyboard.py` chapters; `app/sponsors.py`; `tests/unit/test_sponsors.py`.

## Steps
1. `suggest_slots(project)` pure function; UI and CLI use it.
2. Rules: no long read in the first 60 s; at most one long and one short by default; disclosure visible for paid sponsors; sponsor not between a question and its answer (heuristic: previous paragraph ends with '?'); music not louder than voice under the read.

## Acceptance
- Unit tests for each rule and suggestion on fixtures.

## Verify
```sh
.venv/bin/pytest tests/unit/test_sponsors.py -q
```

## Don't
- Suggestions only; never move a slot without the user.

## Implementer notes
- Suggestions are returned as `sponsor_suggestions` in the shared timeline response
  (UI API and `timeline --json` CLI); editor controls belong to T10.6. Times
  are unsponsored body picture time, including HEAD_PAD, excluding slates.
  Mid-roll chooses the chapter boundary nearest the 35–50% interval (ties
  nearest 42.5%); without chapters, use a paragraph boundary.
- SponsorSlot has no payment field: add backward-compatible `paid=False`.
  Paid reads require disclosure wording in `vars.made`, the visible text in
  bundled sponsor templates. Custom templates cannot be verified statically
  and should produce a disclosure finding for paid reads.
- Music gain under a read is `music_db + sponsor_music_db`; check only when
  music is present. Checks use actual placed segment times, including slates
  and preceding reads; disabled/unplaced reads do not trigger findings.
