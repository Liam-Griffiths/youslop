# T10.3 Sponsor scene templates: short and long
Phase 10 · Size M · Depends on: T10.1, T2.3
*Written 2026-10-31 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the owner's brief; anchors checked against `dev` at that date.*

## Goal
Scene templates for a short read (5–10 s) and a long read (30–60 s) take the sponsor's name, logo, colours, URL, code and word-timed beats; the house short read (mockup 2026-10-31) becomes the first production template; Short-form (9:16) variants exist.

## Why
The mockup proved the engine can land animation on spoken words. Templates turn it into something any sponsor can use.

## Read first
- `static/scene/sponsor/house_short.html`, `docs/brand/mockups/sponsor-house-short.mp4`, `app/scenes.py`, `static/scene/runtime.js`, `app/words.py` (word timings).

## Steps
1. Generalise house_short: beats from the take's word timings (find the sponsor name and "description"/"link" words; fall back to proportional times).
2. `sponsor/short.html` (alias of the approved corkboard house short), `sponsor/long.html` (logo intro, 2–4 B-roll/product slots from the library or footage, lower third with URL and code; optional QR may be omitted, end card).
3. 9:16 variants for Shorts (`short_vertical.html`, `long_vertical.html`), with portrait capture geometry.
4. Lint clean (`scenes.lint`), with a browser-free `scene-check` CLI. Owner override: no captures/renders in the sandbox; reviewer captures quarter-size stills.

## Acceptance
- Each template renders at quarter size with sample vars; text fits at the longest allowed name/URL.
