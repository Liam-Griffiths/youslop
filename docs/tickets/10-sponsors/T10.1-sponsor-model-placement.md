# T10.1 Sponsor segments in the data model and the cut
Phase 10 · Size M · Depends on: T0.7, T4.2, T2.4
*Written 2026-10-31 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the owner's brief; anchors checked against `dev` at that date.*

## Goal
A project holds sponsor slots (`Project.sponsors`), each with a variant (short or long), a placement (pre-roll after the hook, mid-roll at a paragraph boundary or time, end before the outro), its own script, take and scene; `render.timeline()` and `render()` insert the segment at that point and every later time (chapters, titles, cues) shifts by its length.

## Why
The owner wants sponsor reads, short and long, placed anywhere in the video. They are not paragraphs: they have their own look, their own voice line, and must be movable without rewriting the script.

## Read first
- `app/schema.py` (`Project`, `to_dict`/`from_dict` traps in NOTES.md), `app/render.py` `timeline` (~2164), `render` (~1317), `_build_intro`/`_build_outro` (how a non-paragraph segment is built and joined), `_audio_graph` (~1199).
- `app/scenes.py` `ensure_clip`; `static/scene/sponsor/house_short.html` (the 2026-10-31 mockup).
- `app/storyboard.py` (contract), `youtube.chapter_points`.

## Steps
1. `SponsorSlot` dataclass: `id, sponsor_id, variant ('short'|'long'), placement ('pre'|'mid'|'end'), after_slide_id, at_s, script, take (dict, T4.9 rules), template, vars, enabled`. Add to Project in all three places (dataclass, to_dict, from_dict).
2. `timeline()` emits a `sponsor` segment at its slot: `pre` after the hook paragraph(s) (FrameSpec hook length), `mid` after `after_slide_id`, `end` before the outro.
3. Render: build the segment from its scene template and take like an intro card; hard cut in and out (or the format's transition); the music bed dips by `Style.sponsor_music_db` (default -6 dB further) under the read.
4. Storyboard and chapters: a sponsor shows in the storyboard; chapter times after it shift; chapters list gains a "Sponsor" entry (T10.7 uses it).
5. Unit tests: timeline times shift exactly by the segment length; graph golden for a project with one mid-roll.

## Acceptance
- A fixture project with one short mid-roll: `timeline()` shows the segment at the right place, later segments shifted by its length; disabling the slot removes it with no other change.
- Old projects load with `sponsors == []`.

## Verify

## Don't

- Never place a sponsor inside a paragraph.
