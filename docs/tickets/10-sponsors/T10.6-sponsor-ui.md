# T10.6 Sponsors in the editor
Phase 10 · Size M · Depends on: T10.1, T9.10 (or T4.4)
*Written 2026-10-31 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the owner's brief; anchors checked against `dev` at that date.*

## Goal
The Edit stage shows a sponsor lane: add a sponsor, pick short or long, drag to a boundary (snapping to paragraph ends), preview it, toggle it off.

## Why
Placement is visual; the timeline is where it belongs.

## Read first
- `timeline.js` layer rows (T4.4); T10.1 API.

## Steps
1. Lane with sponsor blocks at their times; drag snaps to allowed boundaries; inspector shows sponsor, variant, copy, take.
2. Preview renders the segment at quarter size.

## Acceptance
- Add, move, switch variant, disable and preview all work and persist.

## Verify
```sh
CORKBORED_DATA=$(mktemp -d) .venv/bin/python scripts/ui_snapshots.py --out /tmp/ui
```

## Don't
- Never render the whole cut to preview a sponsor.
