# T15.2 Picture search relevance: subject-aware queries and a relevance gate
Phase 15 · Size M · Depends on: —
*Written 2026-11-08 (Claude Opus 5.5) after building the example video, "The Tube Mail".*

## Goal
Automatic picture gathering returns pictures of the paragraph's subject, or nothing. It never returns an off-topic picture that only shares a keyword.

## Why
For a script about the Paris and Berlin pneumatic post, gathering chose these for 20 of 20 paragraphs:
- photos of a 1901 Paris–Berlin car race ("Die Woche Autorennen Paris-Berlin", used 8 times);
- ESCP business-school campuses ("Escp-Berlin", "Escp-Torino", "Escp-Paris", "Escp-London");
- a garden statue;
- a Citroën 2CV rally car;
- a fishing boat.

"Topped up 19 thin slides from the subject pool" spread the car race across the video. Yet good public-domain material was one query away. Searching by hand for "poste pneumatique Paris", "Rohrpost Berlin" or "carte pneumatique" returned 6–10 public-domain hits each: the 1880s engravings of the network, the Bourse office, the steam plant, real petit bleu cards and Rohrpost envelopes.

## Read first
- `app/media/__init__.py`: `search`, `_ladder`, `gather`, `_top_up`, `_autopick`, `subject_query`.
- `app/media/providers.py` (Wikimedia, Openverse, LoC, Met, NYPL), `app/script_gen.py` (`visual_note` and query writing).

## Steps
1. **Subject terms.** Derive the video's subject terms once from the title, the research dossier and the outline. Here that means "pneumatic post", "poste pneumatique", "Rohrpost", "pneumatic tube" and "petit bleu". Include each language's own term where the dossier gives it.
2. **Paragraph queries.** Each paragraph's queries pair its specifics (a place, a year, an object) with at least one subject term. A paragraph about the Bourse becomes "Bourse Paris poste pneumatique" first, then plain "Palais de la Bourse 1866", and never "Paris Berlin" alone.
3. **Relevance gate.** Score each hit's title, description and categories against the subject terms and the paragraph's nouns. A hit sharing only a place name (for example "Paris" and "Berlin" with no subject term) is rejected for automatic choice. It stays visible in the picker, marked "weak match".
4. **Top-up.** `_top_up` reuses only gated hits, and never the same picture for more than 2 paragraphs. If there aren't enough, the paragraph stays without a picture and gets a "Needs a picture" finding. A wrong picture is worse than a gap.
5. **Licence preference.** Optionally (a project setting, default on for the bundled example and off otherwise) prefer public domain and CC0 over CC BY/BY-SA when relevance is equal.
6. **Fixture test.** Record the provider responses for this script (from `cache/media` in the example data) as a fixture. The test asserts that no automatic pick has a title matching `Autorennen|Escp|Citroen|senneur|Skulptur`, and that at least 10 of 20 paragraphs get a gated, subject-matching picture.

## Acceptance
- The fixture test above passes offline with recorded responses.
- Re-gathering "The Tube Mail" (reviewer, with network) picks subject pictures for most paragraphs and leaves the rest empty. No car races.

## Don't
- No LLM call per picture. The gate is local text scoring; an optional LLM re-rank can come later.
