# T19.2 Screenshots and a WebM trailer from the running app
Phase 19 · Size M · Depends on: T19.1, T15.1 · Worker: Claude Opus 5.5
*Written 2026-11-11 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the owner's brief in `README.md`.*

## Goal
The site shows real screenshots of the running app and a short trailer of
60–90 s. The trailer is encoded as WebM, and every capture can be
regenerated with one command.

## Why
Real screens make few claims and prove each one. Being able to regenerate
them means the site keeps up as the UI changes.

## Read first
- T19.1 and `site/tools/build_images.py`.
- `scripts/ui_snapshots.py`, the existing headless capture of every
  stage.
- `app/stage.py` (`_chromium_flags`, CDP capture).
- `docs/tickets/NOTES.md`: on headless VP9 decode, add
  `--disable-accelerated-video-decode`.
- The bundled example from T15.1, "Under the Street" / "The Tube Mail".
  It's public-domain media only, so it's safe to show.

## Steps
1. **Capture data.**
   - `site/tools/capture.py` imports the example `.corkbored` into a fresh
     temp `CORKBORED_DATA` with `transfer.import_`.
   - It starts the app server on a free port.
   - It drives headless Chromium at 1440×900 with device scale 2.
   - It never touches the owner's data folders.
2. **Screenshots.**
   - Capture one per feature row, choosing states that read at a glance:
     - Plan with sources;
     - the Script editor;
     - Voice with a take's waveform;
     - Visuals with mixed kinds and a block chip;
     - the Blocks library;
     - the Edit timeline;
     - Review & Publish;
     - the clip finder transcript;
     - Settings → local models.
   - Crop to the content area where the full window is noise. Write PNGs to
     `site/media-src/` and run `build_images.py`.
   - Add a light-frame variant only if the app has a light theme by then.
     Otherwise show the dark app inside the light site's soft frame, which
     looks intentional.
3. **Trailer footage.**
   - Record short screen passes through the same flow with CDP
     `Page.startScreencast`, or by recording the virtual display with
     ffmpeg:
     - typing an idea;
     - research arriving;
     - the script;
     - a take recording;
     - pictures filling in;
     - a block animating;
     - the timeline playing;
     - the finished video.
   - Add 3–5 s of the finished example video, using a section preview the
     owner has authorised (see below).
4. **Cut.**
   - Cut it with ffmpeg in `site/tools/trailer.py`, using a declarative edit
     list: clip, in, out, caption.
   - Captions are short lower-left labels in the site's type, made as app
     blocks or drawn PNGs.
   - Use no voiceover. Music is optional and must be CC0 or from the app's
     own bundled packs, with its licence recorded.
5. **Encode.**
   - Main: VP9 WebM, two-pass, 1920×1080 (or 1600×900 if smaller), CRF
     about 34, `-row-mt 1`. Target under 8 MB for 75 s.
   - Mobile: 1280×720 WebM under 4 MB, selected with `<source media>`.
   - Poster: a JPEG/AVIF frame under 60 KB.
   - Optional: an AV1 WebM listed first, if it is at least 25 % smaller at
     equal quality. Check Safari: current Safari plays VP9 WebM. Record what
     was tested in the Log.
6. **Place.**
   - Use `<video muted playsinline loop preload="none" poster>`, which
     starts on visibility with an IntersectionObserver.
   - Offer a "Play with sound" control only if there is music.
   - Video files go under `public/media/` with content-hashed names and
     are served by Vercel with the site (see the README cost model). Check
     that the WebM is served as `video/webm` and that range requests work,
     so seeking doesn't refetch the file.

## Acceptance
- One command regenerates all screenshots: `site/tools/capture.py`.
  `trailer.py` regenerates the trailer from the captured footage.
- Every screenshot and trailer frame comes from the real app and the bundled
  example. No mocked UI, no owner data.
- The trailer is 60–90 s, the main WebM is under 8 MB and the mobile WebM
  under 4 MB. It plays in Chromium and Firefox. The Log records the result
  in Safari, or that it was untested.
- The owner watches the trailer before it ships.

## Owner gates (say before doing)
- The capture runs the app and Chromium for several minutes. The trailer
  encode is two-pass at 1080p. **Both are heavy renders on this machine:
  say before running them.**
- Footage of the finished example is a section preview at quarter size
  unless the owner authorises a full-quality render of that section. Never
  render the whole cut.

## Don't
- Show the owner's projects, channels, keys or file paths.
- Put media in git LFS. Vercel builds would need LFS enabled, and the files
  are small enough not to need it. Keep the encoded outputs small and record
  their sizes in the Log. Only the final encodes are committed; raw footage
  stays out of git.
