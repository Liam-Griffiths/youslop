# T13.4 Style picker: one picker, in the right places, with "Design a block"
Phase 13 · Size M · Depends on: T13.2, T13.3, T12.12b
*Written 2026-11-08 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the owner's style rethink.*

## Goal
One grouped style picker that shows real previews. It sits on Home (start), at the head of Visuals (change, with a summary first) and on the channel page (house style). Review & Publish only shows what will render. "Design a style…" becomes "Design a block…".

## Why
The review found these problems (2026-11-08):
- The picker appears three times. In a project it is buried in Review & Publish, after the work it governs is done.
- Home has both a Format dropdown and a card grid for the same choice.
- One flat grid mixes photographic, drawn, 3D and custom styles, and puts Design and Import tiles among them.
- Hand-drawn icons instead of previews; all-caps mono blurbs.
- Knobs are shown and then declared ignored.
- Hex colours typed as text.

Owner chose "design a block" over Claude drawing a whole slide style.

## Steps
1. **Picker component.** `stylePicker({current, onPick, context})` lists kits in groups: Pictures, Drawn, 3D, Your styles. Each card shows:
   - a real poster: the kit drawn on a sample paragraph, cached, generated like block posters;
   - the name;
   - a one-line blurb in sentence case (not all-caps mono);
   - the `needs` line.

   Hover plays a short motion preview (T12.11 machinery). Below the grid, as plain buttons rather than cards: "Save current as a style…", "Import a style…", "Design a block…".
2. **Home.** Replace the Format dropdown and More options grid with one compact control showing the chosen style card. Clicking it opens the picker in a dialog. Remove "Saved format" from More options: user kits now appear under Your styles.
3. **Visuals header.** A "Style: Documentary · Change" line at the top of Visuals. Change opens the picker, then shows T13.3's dry-run summary, and nothing is applied until Apply is pressed. It links to "Look and block theme" (the T12.12b panel plus the photographic look knobs).
4. **Channel page.** "House look" becomes "House style": the picker, then the look and block theme panels. Music level moves to the channel's Sound settings.
5. **Review & Publish.** The "Look, sound and opening" style picker and look knobs are replaced by a read-only summary: the style, the kinds in use, the theme swatch and the music bed, with "Change in Visuals" and "Change sound" links. Sound controls stay, since they are render-time settings.
6. **Only relevant knobs.** Look knobs are shown only for kinds present in the video: drift for photo kinds, the board hand for board, a cut's look for each cut present. Remove the "ignores the drift…" notes. Colours use the T12.12b swatch inputs.
7. **Design a block.** "Design a block…" opens the Blocks screen's describe flow (`describeBlock`). The finished block lands in Blocks with a "Use in this video" action. The old style designer (`openStyleDesigner`) stays reachable for existing custom styles (edit only), but can no longer be started from the picker.
8. **Snapshots.** Update the routes for Home, the channel page, Visuals and Publish.

## Acceptance
In headless Chromium:
- Starting a video from Home with Casefile creates it with `style.format == 'casefile'` and the kit's look.
- Changing the style in Visuals shows the summary, and Cancel changes nothing.
- Publish shows no style picker.
- The channel page shows House style with block theme.
- "Design a block…" lands on the describe box.

0 console errors. Existing projects open with their current style selected.

## Don't
- Don't remove custom slide-style rendering or editing for existing styles.
- Don't call a real LLM. Kit posters are drawn locally.

## Implementer notes
- `paintMedia` and the Visuals header live in `visuals.js`, not `media.js`.
- Home must apply `kit_id` inside `ops.prepare_create`, before the initialization
  job starts; setting only `format` loses the kit's look and block preferences.
- Built-in kits carry only theme/accent/accent2/grade. Applying one preserves
  drift, crossfade, vignette, board settings and renderer engines (T13.3 review).
- Kit previews use scratch caches, never owner projects or recorded takes.
