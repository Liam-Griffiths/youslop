# T9.8 Visuals stage: one picker for every source
Phase 9 · Size M · Depends on: T9.3, T9.4
*Written 2026-10-31 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the owner's brief; anchors checked against `dev` at that date.*

## Goal
Per picture group, a large preview with a side panel of tabs: Archive (search), Library, Footage (Phase 11 clips), Scenes (templates), Upload; choosing from any tab sets the same slide fields; locking and focal point stay.

## Why
Media, Stage/Cast and uploads are separate places today; with footage arriving, one picker matters.

## Read first
- Media tab code in `static/js/tabs/media.js`, shell routing in `static/js/shell.js` and `static/js/core.js`; `app/media/__init__.py` candidate selection; `library.py`; Phase 11 `footage` provider (stub the tab until T11.6).

## Steps
*Updated 2026-10-31 from the scenario mockups (canvas "Corkbored studio flow",
row "Same six stages, different videos"): Visuals comes after Voice, and its
right panel adapts to the format. Four cases must work:*
- *Documentary with your footage: Footage tab first, best matches by label.*
- *3D explainer with archive: a 3D tab with the cast (models, named parts) and
  the shot planner's options per paragraph, plus Archive and Scenes.*
- *Piece to camera (vlog): a Layout tab per paragraph (full face, split, cutaway
  over voice, picture in picture), jump-cut view of the take (removed parts
  struck through, never deleted), cutaways suggested from words in the take.*
- *Vertical data Short: scene templates with editable variables and a data
  table (from a research source), 9:16 preview, safe-area check.*
*Every case shows a "visual mix" bar (share of each kind) with the format's
rule, and a sequence strip of the whole video at the bottom.*

1. Group list down the left, preview centre, source tabs right.
2. Each tab returns `MediaCandidate`s through the existing choose path.
3. Footage is a labelled stub until T11.6 supplies the picker provider; do not add a provider here. Preserve existing Cast, shot, camera and block editors as reachable sub-areas if the complete merge exceeds this ticket (owner clarification, 2026-11-01).

## Acceptance
