# T9.9 Voice stage redesign
Phase 9 · Size M · Depends on: T9.3, T4.9
*Written 2026-10-31 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the owner's brief; anchors checked against `dev` at that date.*

## Goal
Voice shows every paragraph with its current take, earlier takes (T4.9), record/synthesise buttons, level meter while recording, and the mic setup as a drawer instead of a page section.

## Why
Recording is a focused activity; the current Record tab mixes setup and takes.

## Read first
- `static/js/tabs/record.js`, `static/js/tabs/camera.js`, `static/js/shell.js`, and `static/styles.css`.
- `upload_take`, `_voice_one`, `voice_test`, and the T4.9 history API in `app/main.py`.
- `docs/design/studio-flow.md` §7 “05 Voice” and the full approved `docs/design/mockups/Voice.dc.html` (layout source of truth).

## Steps
*Updated 2026-10-31: Voice now comes before Visuals. For piece-to-camera projects the stage is labelled Camera and records video takes with the prompter (T7.2).*

1. Paragraph list with take status; teleprompter view for the selected paragraph.
2. Record / stop / retake; previous takes listed with play and "use this".
3. Mic setup drawer (existing analysis and voice chain controls).

## Acceptance
- Record, synthesise, retake, restore an earlier take all work; no file is ever deleted.

## Verify
```sh
CORKBORED_DATA=$(mktemp -d) .venv/bin/python scripts/ui_snapshots.py --out /tmp/ui
```
