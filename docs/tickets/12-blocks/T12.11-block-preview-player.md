# T12.11 Block preview player: plays in the app window, expands, scrubs
Phase 12 · Size M · Depends on: T12.8b
*Written 2026-11-08 (Claude Opus 5.5) from owner feedback. Anchors checked against `dev` at that date.*

## Goal
Every block preview plays in the desktop app window. Clicking a block's preview expands it to a large player with play/pause, scrubbing, loop and aspect switching.

## Why
Owner, 2026-11-08: "the block preview … doesn't animate or expand or even play for me". The likely cause is in the code: `block_library.preview(kind='motion')` writes **H.264 `.mp4`** (`blocks.render` outputs `.mp4`), and the app window on a Linux desktop has no H.264 decoder. Every other in-app preview is VP9 `.webm` for exactly this reason (see the comment above `mute = …_mute.webm` in `render.py` and `ffutil.video_args`/`is_preview`). Headless Chromium plays H.264, which is why our review probes missed it. Beyond the codec, the motion is capped at 6 s, the detail panel shows it at card size and nothing expands.

## Read first
- `app/block_library.py`: `preview`, `preview_file`, `make_posters`.
- `app/blocks.py`: `render` (the `out = html.with_suffix('.mov' if overlay else '.mp4')` line, `max_seconds`).
- `app/ffutil.py`: `video_args`, `audio_args`, `is_preview`, `VP9_SPEED`.
- `static/js/tabs/blocks_browser.js`: `requestBlockPreview`, card hover/Space handling, `#block-play`, the detail panel, `blockPosterArrived` (the poster poll must not repaint a playing video).
- `docs/design/studio-flow.md` (dialogs, focus, reduced motion).

## Steps
1. **Codec.** Motion previews are VP9 `.webm` (via `ffutil.video_args` on a `.webm` path), whether `blocks.render` encodes directly or a transcode step runs afterwards. `preview_file` returns `.webm` for motion. Overlay previews stay alpha: flatten them onto a neutral checker or a sample still for the preview only (see step 4), never into the cached overlay `.mov` that renders use. Old `.mp4` preview cache files are simply never looked up again; leave them.
2. **Full length.** Preview the block's default duration, not a 6 s cap, up to 15 s. Keep quarter size (480×270, or 270×480 for 9:16) for the card hover. The expanded player requests a half-size render (960×540) on demand, with the same one-in-flight sequencing as today.
3. **Card behaviour.** Hover (after 400 ms) or Space plays the card's motion inline, muted and looping. Leaving the card or pressing Space again stops it and shows the poster. Reduced motion means no hover autoplay; click still plays. A spinner or "Rendering preview…" label shows while the first render runs (cold is about 1 s).
4. **Expanded player.** Clicking the detail panel's preview, or a new "Expand" button, opens a large dialog: video at up to 80% of the viewport and the block's aspect, play/pause, a scrub bar showing the time, loop toggle, and an aspect switch (16:9, 9:16, plus 1:1 where declared). For overlay blocks there is a "Show over" switch with three options: a neutral checker, the sample still and (when opened from a project) the paragraph's own picture. Esc closes it and focus returns to the trigger. The dialog uses the app's existing dialog styling (`studioDialog` from `palette.js` or the shared modal); no new colours.
5. **Errors.** If a render fails, the panel shows the error inline with a Retry button, not a blank box. If the video element raises `error` (an unsupported codec, a missing file), it says so in words instead of failing silently.
6. **Snapshot route.** Add a `ui_snapshots.py` step or route that opens the expanded player on a built-in block, so regressions show up in the 0-console-error run.

## Acceptance
- `ffprobe` on a fresh motion preview reports `vp9` in a WebM container. A test asserts `preview_file(..., 'motion').suffix == '.webm'`.
- A motion preview of `title_card` lasts its default 5 s, not 6 s capped or shorter.
- In headless Chromium: hovering a card swaps the poster for a playing `<video>` (`!paused`, `currentTime` advancing). Clicking the preview opens the dialog. Scrubbing sets `currentTime`. Esc closes the dialog and focus returns to the trigger. There are 0 console errors.
- An overlay block's expanded preview shows the block over the checker, not over a black ground.

## Don't
- Don't change render outputs or segment proxies, and don't change overlay `.mov` caching.
- No per-frame CDP. Previews still go through the scene builder.
- Don't add a JS video library. Use a plain `<video>` with our own controls.
