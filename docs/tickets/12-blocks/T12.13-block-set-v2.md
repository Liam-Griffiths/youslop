# T12.13 Block set v2: forty new blocks, in five batches
Phase 12 · Size L (five Codex runs) · Depends on: T12.12a (tokens), T12.11 (previews)
*Written 2026-11-08 (Claude Opus 5.5) from owner feedback. The inspiration survey was of the HyperFrames registry (heygen-com/hyperframes, `registry/blocks`, Apache-2.0, 210 blocks).*

## Goal
Add about 40 original Corkbored blocks in five themed batches. Each one passes `block check` in both aspects, draws from block-theme tokens, and ships with a poster and a motion preview.

## Why
Owner, 2026-11-08: "let's design more blocks, take inspiration from the hyperframes registry". We have 39 blocks (`static/blocks/`). The registry shows the categories creators reach for that we lack: more lower-third styles, counters and progress stats, route and bubble maps, chat, post and notification cards, code and terminal, picture frames and stacks.

## Clean-room rule (read this first)
**Inspiration only. Don't copy or adapt HyperFrames code, markup, CSS, SVG paths, names or layouts.** Don't open their block sources while authoring. Design each block from the one-line brief below, in our own vocabulary. Our blocks are GPL-3.0-or-later like the rest (packs are MIT, T8.3). Mixing in Apache-2.0 code would need attribution and notices we don't want in block files. No platform look-alikes: no X, Reddit, TikTok, Instagram, Slack, ChatGPT, Claude, Apple or YouTube chrome, logos, colours or names. A post card is a generic post card in our theme.

## Read first
- `docs/blocks/format.md` (motion presets, layers, `data-capture="frames"` and `budget`, overlay mode), `docs/blocks/block.schema.json`, `docs/scene-builder.md`.
- `docs/tickets/12-blocks/T12.3-core-set.md` and its Log (how v1 was batched and checked), `docs/blocks/briefs/`.
- Existing family bases (`chapter/text_base.html`, `number_slam/data_base.html`, `step_list/explainer_base.html`), `app/core_data_blocks.py`, `app/core_explainer_blocks.py`.
- T12.12a's Log: the token names. New blocks use only tokens: no literal colours or font families.

## Rules for every new block
- Use our motion vocabulary: layers plus presets (fade, rise, fly-left/right, scale-in, wipe, words, blur-in, pop) with `data-motion-duration` and `data-motion-exit`. Counters, typing and split-flap may use `data-capture="frames"` only inside their arrival window and within `budget`; record the budget used in the Log. No continuous shader or particle animation, and no per-frame CDP outside a declared window (NOTES.md "Scene rendering speed").
- `block.json` needs a plain `description` that says what it's for (T12.8 style), plus `category`, `tags`, realistic defaults, `max_length` limits that fit both aspects, and `overlay` where the block works over a picture.
- Data blocks take a table through the T12.4 path when they show figures, and they get the number-match check.
- Pictures come from `image` variables with a local sample in the block folder that we drew or generated, never fetched.
- Every block passes `block check` in 16:9 and 9:16, gets a poster via `make_posters`, and is listed in the batch's contact sheet `docs/blocks/v2-<batch>.png`.

## Batches (one Codex run each; do only the batch you are given)

**A. Names and titles (8).**
- `lower_third_kicker`: small kicker line above a name; overlay.
- `lower_third_underline`: name with an accent underline that draws in; overlay.
- `lower_third_pill`: rounded pill with name and role; overlay.
- `lower_third_reveal`: name revealed by a sliding mask; overlay.
- `lower_third_portrait`: card with a small round picture, name and role; overlay.
- `part_title`: large part number and title with a hairline.
- `split_flap_title`: a departures-board title that flips into place, using a frames window.
- `logo_sting`: the channel's logo or wordmark arriving, then holding.

**B. Numbers (8).**
- `counter`: a number counting up to a value with prefix and suffix, using a frames window.
- `progress_stat`: a bar filling to a percentage with a label.
- `spec_list`: label and value rows arriving in turn.
- `unit_chart`: "1 in N", with a grid of simple icons and some highlighted.
- `ranking_list`: ranked rows with values and bars.
- `calendar_grid`: days in a month grid with marked days.
- `delta_card`: before and after numbers with the change and its direction.
- `take_waveform`: the paragraph's own narration waveform drawn as a line, with a quote under it. It is computed from the take at prepare time, offline, and falls back to a sample.

**C. Places (6).** Needs vendored country outlines: the reviewer adds Natural Earth 1:110m admin-0 (public domain) under `static/blocks/_geo/` before this batch runs. If the file is missing, stop BLOCKED.
- `route_map`: an arc drawn from A to B on a world or region outline, with labels.
- `bubble_map`: sized bubbles at latitude/longitude points from a table.
- `region_map`: countries shaded by value from a table, with a legend.
- `place_pin`: an overlay with a place name, coordinates and a small locator inset.
- `distance_card`: two places, the distance and travel time.
- `timezone_strip`: several cities with their local times.

**D. Screens (10).** Generic and themed, with no platform chrome.
- `chat_thread`: messages appear in turn with sender names.
- `post_card`: a generic social post with a handle, text, optional picture and counts.
- `comment_card`: a single highlighted comment; overlay.
- `notification_stack`: notifications dropping in one by one.
- `news_ticker`: a crawling headline band; overlay. It may use one long fly-left layer.
- `search_box`: a query typed into a search bar, using a frames window, then results.
- `code_typing`: code typed into an editor panel with syntax colours from tokens.
- `code_diff`: removed and added lines highlighted in turn.
- `terminal`: a command, then output lines.
- `flow_steps`: a horizontal or vertical flow of boxes and arrows, revealed one by one.

**E. Pictures and frames (8).**
- `photo_stack`: up to four pictures dropping onto a pile.
- `filmstrip`: three to five pictures sliding past in a strip.
- `polaroid_pair`: two captioned prints side by side.
- `viewfinder`: an overlay with camera framing, REC, a static timecode and corner marks.
- `archive_frame`: a picture in an archival mount with a catalogue label.
- `triptych`: three pictures in panels with captions.
- `zoom_detail`: a picture with a magnified inset of one region (with `focal_x`/`focal_y`).
- `caption_strip`: a full-width caption band for documentary subtitles; overlay.

## Acceptance (per batch)
- Every new block passes `block check` in both aspects, and the overlay ones pass on transparency.
- Posters exist, and a motion preview of each plays (T12.11 player) with 0 console errors.
- The contact sheet `docs/blocks/v2-<batch>.png` is committed.
- No literal colour or font-family strings in the new `block.html` files (T12.12a check passes).
- `grep -ri hyperframes static/blocks` finds nothing.
