# T11.3 Quality signals and duplicates
Phase 11 · Size S · Depends on: T11.2
*Written 2026-10-31 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the owner's brief; anchors checked against `dev` at that date.*

## Goal
Every clip gets blur, shake, exposure and audio-level scores, a usable/unusable flag with reasons, and near-duplicates are grouped with a suggested best take.

## Why
Raw shoots are full of misfires (pocket shots, focus hunting, wind). Hiding them saves the user an hour.

## Read first
- T11.2 clips: `app/footage.py` (`Clip`, `detect_shots`, `process_and_detect`),
  `tests/unit/test_shots.py`, and `ffutil.run` in `app/ffutil.py`;
  Pillow/numpy availability in `.venv`.

## Steps
1. Blur: variance of Laplacian on 3 frames. Shake: mean frame-to-frame shift (phase correlation on downscaled frames). Exposure: histogram clipping. Audio: RMS and wind-band energy.
2. Duplicates: perceptual hash of middle frames + duration similarity; group; best = least blur/shake.
3. Thresholds in one place, unit-tested on synthetic cases.

## Acceptance
- Synthetic blurred/shaky/black clips are flagged; duplicates grouped.

## Verify
```sh
.venv/bin/pytest tests/unit/test_footage_quality.py -q
```

## Don't
- Never delete flagged clips; flags only.

## Implementer notes
- Quality analysis runs after detection in `process_and_detect`, on proxy clip
  spans only. `analyze_quality` is also a synchronous worker. It saves the
  manifest after all scores and duplicate groups are complete; retries recompute
  scores and retain clip IDs and media. Missing fields default safely on old
  manifests, and status exposes the saved fields.
- `Clip.quality` stores mean Laplacian variance (`blur`, higher is sharper),
  mean adjacent-frame phase-correlation displacement (`shake`, pixels at
  160×90 and 8 fps), dark/bright pixel fractions, linear full-scale `audio_rms`,
  and the fraction of audio energy below 200 Hz (`wind_fraction`). Blur and
  exposure use three distributed samples; shake uses all sampled pairs.
  No audio stream yields null audio scores without flagging silent B-roll.
  Audio streams with very low/high RMS or dominant low-band energy get reasons.
- `usable` and `reasons` live inside `quality`; these are heuristic flags,
  never deletion or exclusion. Low texture, intentional pans and bass-heavy
  audio can trigger flags. Thresholds live together in `footage_quality.py`.
- DCT perceptual hashes of middle frames, a mean-luma guard (to distinguish
  flat black/white), and relative duration similarity form connected duplicate
  groups. `duplicate_group` is the smallest member ID; `best_take` minimizes
  inverse sharpness plus shake, normalized by their thresholds, with ID ties.
  Singletons have empty group/best fields. Nothing modifies source footage.
