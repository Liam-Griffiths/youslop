# T11.3 Quality signals and duplicates
Phase 11 · Size S · Depends on: T11.2
*Written 2026-10-31 (Claude Opus 5.5) from the owner's brief; anchors checked against `dev` at that date.*

## Goal
Every clip gets blur, shake, exposure and audio-level scores, a usable/unusable flag with reasons, and near-duplicates are grouped with a suggested best take.

## Why
Raw shoots are full of misfires (pocket shots, focus hunting, wind). Hiding them saves the user an hour.

## Read first
- T11.2 clips; Pillow/numpy availability in `.venv`.

## Steps
1. Blur: variance of Laplacian on 3 frames. Shake: mean frame-to-frame shift (phase correlation on downscaled frames). Exposure: histogram clipping. Audio: RMS and wind-band energy.
2. Duplicates: perceptual hash of middle frames + duration similarity; group; best = least blur/shake.
3. Thresholds in one place, unit-tested on synthetic cases.

## Acceptance
- Synthetic blurred/shaky/black clips are flagged; duplicates grouped.

## Verify
```sh
.venv/bin/pytest tests/unit/test_footage_quality.py -q
```

## Don't
- Never delete flagged clips; flags only.
