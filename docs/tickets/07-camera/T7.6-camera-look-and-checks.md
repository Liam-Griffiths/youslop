# T7.6 Camera look and checks
Phase 7 · Size S · Depends on: T7.3, T1.5

## Goal
Camera footage is gently corrected for exposure and colour cast so takes
recorded at different times match, and the check layer flags camera
problems before render: too dark, blown highlights, framing, sync drift, and
talking heads held too long.

## Why
Takes recorded on different days, or in changing daylight, cut together
badly. Most problems are measurable in seconds with ffmpeg, without a model
call.

## Read first
- T7.1 camera take; T7.3 `_camera_look`.
- `app/check.py` (T1.5): rule shape, `Finding`, `fix` kinds.
- `FrameSpec` (T1.1): `max_gap_*` for dead stretches.

## Steps
1. **Measure on upload** (T7.1's processing, one extra ffmpeg pass on a
   1 fps sample): mean luma, the share of clipped highlights (luma > 250),
   and the average colour cast (`signalstats`: `YAVG`, `UAVG`, `VAVG`).
   Store as `take["measure"]`.
2. **Gentle correction** in `_camera_look`: bring mean luma toward a target
   (around 110 on 0-255) with `eq=brightness:contrast`, and neutralise a cast
   with `colorbalance`, each capped so it can never do more than a small
   push. A per-take "look" toggle: auto (default) or as shot.
3. **Matching.** Within one project, correct every camera take toward the
   median of the project's takes rather than a fixed target, so a whole
   video looks shot in one session.
4. **Check rules** (T1.5 style, each a function with a positive and a
   negative test):
   | code | level | when |
   |---|---|---|
   | `camera_dark` | P1 | mean luma under 60 after correction |
   | `camera_blown` | P1 | over 3 % of pixels clipped |
   | `camera_sync` | P0 | the audio/video start offset measured in T7.1 is over 80 ms and was not corrected |
   | `camera_low_res` | P2 | under 1280 px wide |
   | `camera_held` | P2 | a camera stretch longer than `frame.max_gap_after_s` with no cut, punch or cutaway |
   | `camera_no_focal` | P2 | no focal point set and the take is not 16:9 (the crop may cut the face) |
5. **Focal point.** The camera take's frame in the Record review step (T7.2)
   lets you click the face to set `focal`, as pictures already do.

## Acceptance
- Two takes of the same person, one darker, preview at visibly matched
  brightness.
- Each new check fires on a crafted bad take and stays quiet on a good one.

## Verify
```sh
.venv/bin/pytest tests/unit/test_camera_checks.py -q
```

## Don't
- No face detection library or model; the focal point is clicked by the
  person.
- No strong grading; the correction is a small push, never a look.
