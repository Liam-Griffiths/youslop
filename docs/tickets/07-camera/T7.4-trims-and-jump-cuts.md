# T7.4 Trims and jump cuts on camera takes
Phase 7 · Size M · Depends on: T7.3, T4.4

## Goal
A camera take can be edited without re-recording: trim its ends, cut out
pauses and fluffed lines, and get the standard talking-head jump cut with an
alternating punch-in, with picture and sound always cut together.

## Why
Unedited piece-to-camera is the clearest sign of an amateur video. Cutting
out pauses and stumbles, with a slight zoom change at each cut so the jump
reads as deliberate, is the editing every talking-head channel does.

## Read first
- T7.1's camera take dict; T7.3's `_camera_segment`.
- `app/audio.py`: `_levels` (20 ms envelope), `speech_span`.
- T0.6 words (estimates for camera takes; see NOTES.md).
- The Timeline layers and drag editing in `static/js/timeline/` (the former
  `static/timeline.js` is empty); `docs/design/studio-flow.md` first.
- `FrameSpec` (T1.1): `punch_min`, `punch_max`, `snap_on_cut`.

## Steps
1. **Edit list, not new files.** A camera take gains
   `"cuts": [{"a": s, "b": s, "by": "hand"|"auto"}, ...]`: ranges (seconds in
   the take) to remove, each marked with who added it (T7.5's Auto-edit
   relies on `by` to undo only its own cuts). Nothing
   is re-encoded at edit time; the take's effective duration is its duration
   minus the cuts. `duration` used everywhere for timing must become the
   **effective** duration (one helper, `take_duration(take)`).
2. **Apply cuts in both places.** Audio: the narration bed builds the
   paragraph from the take's WAV with the cuts removed, joining with a 10 ms
   crossfade so there is no click (`atrim` pieces plus `acrossfade=d=0.01`).
   Include 5 ms of source context at each internal piece edge so crossfade
   overlap does not shorten the effective duration or drift against picture.
   Picture: `_camera_segment` builds the same pieces with `trim` and
   `concat`. One function returns the list of kept ranges; both use it.
3. **Punch-in.** At each cut, alternate the framing between 1.0 and a punch
   from `frame.punch_min..punch_max` (default about 1.15), anchored on the
   take's `focal` point, so consecutive pieces differ in size. A
   `"punch": false` flag on the take turns it off.
4. **Suggestions.** `camera.suggest_cuts(take) -> list[{a, b}]`: pauses
   longer than 0.6 s inside the speech (from `_levels`), trimmed to leave
   0.15 s either side. Shown as dashed ranges in the Timeline; one click
   accepts each, or "accept all". Never applied automatically (T7.5's
   Auto-edit button is the one-click way to accept them all, marked
   `by: auto` so it can be undone).
5. **Timeline editing.** In the camera paragraph's row, drag to select a
   range and press Delete to add a cut; click a cut to restore it. Trim
   handles on the take's ends. Snapping as in T4.5 (frames and word
   boundaries).
6. **Tests:** kept ranges are the complement of the cuts; effective
   duration; audio and picture piece lists are identical; suggestions on a
   synthetic take with two 1 s silences find both.

## Acceptance
- On a real camera take, accepting the suggested cuts removes the pauses;
  the preview plays with lips in sync across every cut and a visible size
  change at each one.
- Restoring a cut brings the original back exactly (nothing was re-encoded).

## Don't
- No automatic removal of anything; suggestions only.
- Do not re-encode or overwrite the take's files when editing.
- No detection of repeated lines or fluffs by speech recognition (T7.8,
  the stretch goal, may add it).

## Implementer notes
*Written 2026-10-26.*

- Every cut changes the paragraph's length, so it moves every later
  paragraph. That is correct (the voice is the clock, and the voice got
  shorter), but it invalidates proxies after the edited paragraph. The proxy
  key already hashes each slide dict, so the take's `cuts` list changes the
  key automatically; check that later segments' keys change too (they
   must include preceding effective durations so later keys change too).
- Word times after cuts: re-base `words` by subtracting the removed time
  before each word; drop words inside a cut. Do it in one helper used by
  the storyboard, check and the Timeline.
- The 1 s keyframe interval from T7.1 is what makes cut-and-concat cheap at
  preview size. For the full render, re-encode the camera segment anyway
  (it is graded), so keyframes do not matter there.
