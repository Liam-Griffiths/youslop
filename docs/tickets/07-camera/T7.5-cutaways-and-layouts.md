# T7.5 Cutaways and layouts over camera
Phase 7 · Size M · Depends on: T7.4, T2.4

## Goal
While a camera paragraph's voice keeps playing, the picture can cut away to
B-roll (a picture, clip, diagram or scene) for a stretch, or share the frame
with it (picture-in-picture or side by side), and captions can sit over the
camera. An **Auto-edit** button applies every suggested cut and cutaway in
one click as a rough cut you then adjust.

## Why
Talking heads that never cut away feel long. The standard grammar is: face
to make the point, cutaway to show it, back to face. Corkbored already has the
pictures and scenes; this lets them sit over a camera voice.

## Read first
- T7.3 `_camera_segment`, T7.4 kept ranges.
- `app/render.py`: how a photographic segment is built from a chosen picture
  (`_chosen`, `_cover_crop`, the Ken Burns chain).
- T2.4 / T2.3: `scenes.ensure_clip` for scene cutaways.
- T5.1: the captions overlay (alpha MOV), if it has landed.

## Steps
1. **Data.** A camera take gains
   `"overlays": [{"at": s, "dur": s, "kind": "cutaway"|"pip"|"split", "source": {...}}]`
   in take time (after cuts), where `source` is a media candidate id from the
   slide's candidates, or a scene template plus context. Patched through
   `patch_slide`.
2. **Cutaway** replaces the picture for its span with the source,
   rendered by the existing photographic or scene path at that span's
   length, joined with hard cuts (no dissolve on a cutaway).
3. **Picture-in-picture**: the source full frame with the camera in a
   rounded corner box (`overlay` with a scaled, cropped camera), or the
   reverse; position from `AvatarSpec`-style options. **Split**: camera
   left, source right, both cover-cropped to half width.
4. **Captions over camera**: when T5.1 has landed, the essay captions
   overlay works on camera segments too (same alpha clip, same timing).
5. **Timeline.** Overlays show in the camera paragraph's row as blocks you
   can drag and resize (T4.5 mechanics); "Add cutaway here" in the
   inspector opens the Media search for this paragraph.
6. **Suggestions.** The cue planner can propose cutaways: when a camera
   paragraph mentions something the media search can find, propose a
   cutaway at that word (uses existing `plan_visuals` output; one prompt
   change, behind the same review-before-apply flow as T3.3).
7. **Auto-edit** (owner request, 2026-10-26). One button, on the Timeline
   and on each camera paragraph's inspector, labelled "Auto-edit" with the
   scope shown ("all camera paragraphs" or "this paragraph"). It:
   - accepts every suggested cut from `camera.suggest_cuts` (T7.4, plus
     T7.8's fluff suggestions when that exists);
   - asks the planner for cutaway suggestions (step 6) for paragraphs that
     have none yet, then applies them, skipping any whose media cannot be
     found or downloaded;
   - marks everything it adds with `"by": "auto"` (cuts and overlays alike),
     drawn with a distinct dashed style on the Timeline;
   - never removes or changes anything added by hand (`"by": "hand"`), and
     re-running it only fills gaps;
   - offers "Undo auto-edit", which removes every `by: auto` item in its
     scope and nothing else;
   - finishes by running the check (T1.5) and showing the result, then
     moves the playhead to the first changed paragraph.
   Proxies for the changed segments rebuild lazily, as for any edit.
8. **Tests:** segment graph golden for one cutaway and one PIP (paths
   normalised); overlay times clamp to the take's effective duration;
   auto-edit applies all suggestions, leaves a hand-made cut untouched,
   re-running adds nothing new, and undo removes exactly the `by: auto`
   items (LLM mocked).

## Acceptance
- A camera paragraph with one cutaway previews: face, cut to the picture on
  the named word, back to face; the voice is continuous throughout.
- Auto-edit on a project with three camera paragraphs produces a watchable
  rough cut in one click; "Undo auto-edit" returns it exactly to how it was.

## Don't
- Nothing is applied automatically except when the creator presses
  Auto-edit, and everything it adds is marked and can be undone in one step.
- No new media sources; use the slide's own candidates and scenes.

## Implementer notes
*Written 2026-10-26.*

- Build the segment as pieces in take time: camera pieces (T7.4 kept ranges
  with punch-ins) and overlay pieces, each rendered to the right length,
  then one `concat`. Picture-in-picture and split are single pieces with an
  `overlay`/`hstack` inside them.
- A cutaway hides the punch-in alternation; continue the alternation across
  it so the face does not come back at the same size it left.
- Auto-edit spends tokens only on the cutaway suggestions (one planner call
  per batch of paragraphs, as `plan_cues` batches 8); cuts are free. Cache
  the suggestions on the take so re-running or undoing never calls the
  model again unless the narration changed.
- Keep the camera's audio as the only voice: overlay sources are always
  rendered silent (`-an`), including video clips with their own sound.
