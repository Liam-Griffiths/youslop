# T7.7 Talking-head videos end to end
Phase 7 · Size S · Depends on: T7.2, T7.3, T7.4

## Goal
A whole video can be made to camera: a new-episode option sets every
paragraph on camera, the script is written to be spoken to a lens, the
Record tab opens in camera mode with the prompter, and the old single
presenter clip is folded into the new model.

## Why
Some videos are entirely piece-to-camera (owner, 2026-10-26). With T7.1 to
T7.6 in place, this is mostly defaults and wording, plus retiring the older,
narrower presenter feature.

## Read first
- `app/schema.py`: `new_project`, `Presenter`.
- `app/script_gen.py`: `NARRATOR_SYSTEM`, `_write_section`, `import_script`
  (faithful and adapted modes).
- `static/app.js`: the new-episode form (`state.newTest`, minutes, research
  select) and the presenter upload UI (grep `presenter`).
- `app/render.py`: `_build_presenter`, `_render_presenter`, and where the
  presenter clip is placed (`position`).

## Steps
1. **New-episode option** "Delivery: Voice-over | On camera | Mixed".
   On camera sets `on_camera = True` on every paragraph as it is written;
   Mixed leaves it per paragraph (the planner may suggest intro and sign-off
   on camera).
2. **Script for camera.** When delivery is On camera, add a short block to
   the writer's prompt: direct address ("you"), sentences that can be said
   in one breath, no reading out of figures that need a chart (move those
   to cutaways). Keep `NARRATOR_SYSTEM` unchanged; the block is additive and
   is a FrameSpec-style setting, not a new system prompt.
3. **Record defaults.** The Record tab opens in camera mode with the prompter
   for these projects.
4. **Presenter migration.** Keep old projects rendering exactly as before.
   In the UI, replace "Upload a camera clip" with "Add a paragraph on
   camera", which creates a new paragraph at the chosen position with the
   uploaded clip as its camera take (T7.1's file drop). Existing presenter
   clips keep working through the old path; show a one-line note offering to
   convert one into a camera paragraph.
5. **Tests:** a new On camera project has every paragraph `on_camera`; the
   prompt for that delivery contains the camera block; an old project with a
   presenter clip renders the same segment graph as before (golden).

## Don't
- Do not delete the presenter code or old presenter files; they keep
  working until a later ticket removes them by choice.
- No change to the voice-over default for new episodes.

## Implementer notes
*Written 2026-10-26.*

- `import_script` with `fidelity="faithful"` keeps the author's words. For
  an On camera essay, the only change worth making is splitting long
  sentences at natural breath points; do not let the camera block override
  faithful mode's "keep my words" rule. Apply the camera block only to
  generated and adapted scripts.
- The marketing plan (`docs/marketing.md`) assumes the owner narrates in
  their own voice; this ticket makes "in their own face" equally easy.
