# T7.3 Camera paragraphs in the render
Phase 7 · Size M · Depends on: T7.1, T0.5

## Goal
A paragraph on camera renders as its camera footage, full frame, in the
channel's look, timed by the same take-plus-gap rule as every other
paragraph, in the full render, the segment preview and the Timeline.

## Why
Once the data exists (T7.1), the renderer is what makes it a video. Doing it
at the segment level means proxies, chapter cards, the audio mix, check and
review all keep working.

## Read first
- `app/render.py`: `_slide_durations`, `_picture_groups` and
  `schema.group_slides` (picture runs), `_render_segment` and T0.5's
  `_segment_graph`, `_render_presenter` (~829: the existing camera look:
  cover crop to a focal point, grade at half strength, vignette at 0.6),
  `_proxy_key`, `timeline()`.
- `app/audio.py`: `build_narration` / `_concat_takes` (takes the active take
  per slide).
- T7.1: `Project.active_take(slide)`, the camera take dict.

## Steps
1. **Grouping.** A paragraph on camera always starts its own picture group
   and never shares one (a camera paragraph is its own shot). Put the rule in
   `schema.group_slides`, next to the chapter rule.
2. **Narration.** `_concat_takes` must use `Project.active_take(slide)`, so a
   camera paragraph's cleaned voice goes into the bed. Nothing else in the
   audio changes, which keeps picture and voice in sync by the existing
   rule.
3. **Segment.** In `_render_segment`, before the format branches: if the
   group's slide is on camera, render
   `_camera_segment(project, slide, seg_len, out, size, fps)`:
   - Input: the take's `video`. The voice starts `T/2` into the segment file
     (plus `HEAD_PAD` on the first), exactly like any segment, so start the
     picture `T/2` (plus pad) **before** the first word by pre-rolling into
     the raw recording if it exists, else holding the first frame.
   - After the last word, use the take's real `tail` frames, then hold the
     last frame (`tpad=stop_mode=clone`) if the gap is longer.
   - Look: reuse `_render_presenter`'s chain (cover crop to `focal`, half
     grade, light vignette). Factor that chain into `_camera_look(style,
     focal, size)` and use it in both places.
   - Titles and chapter cards work as on any photographic segment.
4. **Proxy and preview.** `_proxy_key` includes the camera take's `video`
   file stat and `focal`. "Preview this bit" on a camera paragraph renders
   its proxy at quarter size like any other.
5. **Timeline.** Camera paragraphs draw in the pictures lane with a camera
   marker and a frame thumbnail from their own video.
6. **Tests:** grouping (a camera paragraph is always alone); `_concat_takes`
   picks the camera take; `_segment_graph`-style golden for one camera
   segment (inputs and filter string, paths normalised as in T0.5).

## Acceptance
- A project with one camera paragraph between two voice-over paragraphs
  previews with the cut to camera on the right word and back again, lips in
  sync (check at quarter speed on the proxy).
- Existing goldens for non-camera segments are unchanged.

## Don't

- Do not change `Project.presenter` rendering.
- Preview one segment at a time; never the whole cut.

## Implementer notes
*Written 2026-10-26.*

- The pre-roll in step 3 matters. Every segment file starts `T/2` before
  its paragraph's voice (NOTES.md, timing model). If the camera clip simply
  starts at frame 0 of the trimmed take, the picture runs `T/2` early
  against the voice. Either pre-roll from the raw file (`trimmed[0] - T/2`,
  available because the person was already in frame before speaking) or
  pad the head with the first frame. Prefer pre-roll; it looks natural.
- For drawn-cut formats (`cuts.is_cut`), a camera paragraph must still go
  through `_camera_segment`, not the cut's drawer. The join is a one-frame
  hard cut for those formats, so `T/2` is half a frame there.
- `HOLD_STILL` (`loop=loop=-1:size=1`) is for still images; for video use
  `tpad`. Do not loop camera footage.
