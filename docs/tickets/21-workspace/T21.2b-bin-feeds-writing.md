# T21.2b Bin notes and media feed research, script writing and picture fill
Phase 21 · Size M · Depends on: T21.2, T21.3

## Goal
What the creator drops in the bin is used: notes and links become research
material the script writer cites, labelled pictures and clips are offered
first when pictures are filled, and Script's inspector shows relevant bin
notes next to the facts.

## Read first
- `app/research.py` (`gather`, the dossier shape: facts, sources),
  `app/script_gen.py` (where research enters prompts), `app/media/providers.py`
  and `library.search` (provider pattern), `app/media/relevance.py` (T15.2 gate).

## Steps
1. **Notes → research.** A note or link item becomes a source in the dossier
   with `where: "Bin note"` / its URL; note text is passed through the same
   fact extraction as pasted sources when "Use in research" is on (default
   on for notes, per item toggle). Link items are fetched only through the
   existing research fetcher when research runs; no new network path.
   Creator-written facts are marked `src: "creator"` and outrank web facts
   when they disagree (the writer is told so in the prompt, one line).
2. **Media → picture fill.** A `bin` provider tried before Library and
   archives, matching paragraph queries against title, labels and note.
   Labels count as strong matches. Respect the relevance gate.
3. **Script inspector:** under Facts, "From your bin": notes whose labels or
   text match the paragraph; click inserts a reference or opens the note.
4. Tests with monkeypatched `complete_json`: a bin note fact appears in the
   prompt and dossier; bin provider ranks a labelled image first.

## Acceptance
- A note "The first train ran on 10 Jan 1863" dropped in the bin shows in
  the dossier and is cited by a (mocked) script run.
- A bin picture labelled "post office" fills a paragraph about the post
  office before any archive search runs.
