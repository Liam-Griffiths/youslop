## Implementer notes
- Keep the ordered `project_layers` drawing view and legacy `layers` role dict.
  Reuse `layers.patch_flags` to preserve materialized fallback ids and order.
- Existing `?hide=` supports only coarse roles and excludes Pictures, Voice,
  Captions and Sponsors. Saved hidden flags currently have no runtime effect.
  Titles also carry essay caption spans. Define id/legacy-role precedence
  carefully when a saved track id happens to equal another role name.
- Do not clear takes or narration to mute voice: those fields determine duration,
  word anchors and camera edits. Preserve cue placement and repeated-word anchors
  when filtering, just as graphics ordering resolves anchors before sorting.
- Stings may belong to a channel and the outro spec is wrapped. Music is a
  filename with a companion layer reference. Sponsor/slate proxy paths and clip
  projects need explicit coverage as well as ordinary picture proxies.
