# T2.9 Move captions, boards, diagrams and custom slides onto the builder
Phase 2 · Size M · Depends on: T2.8
*Written 2026-11-01 (Claude Opus 5.5).*

## Goal
Essay captions, board templates, diagrams and custom slides (JSON spec and sanitised
HTML) render through `scene_builder.render`. Their private compositors are deleted.

## Why
Three copies of the plate pattern exist today (`scenes.caption_plates` with the
essay overlay in `render._segment_graph`, `board_scenes.render`,
`custom_scenes.render`, plus `diagram_scenes.render`). One engine is easier to keep
fast and correct.

## Read first
- T2.8 and `docs/scene-builder.md`.
- The four renderers named above and their callers in `app/render.py` and
  `app/board.py`.
- Review logs of T5.1, T5.2a, T5.2b, T5.2c, T5.2d (what was measured and fixed).

## Steps
1. Mark layers in `essay/captions.html`, `board/*.html`, `board/diagram.html` and
   `slides/custom.html` with the T2.8 attributes. The board write-on is the `wipe`
   preset; custom-slide rise/fly/pop map to presets; the pen-like soft nib stays.
2. Replace each private renderer's body with a call to `scene_builder.render`. Keep
   their public function names as thin wrappers so callers do not change.
3. Delete the duplicated plate/crop/overlay code and `board_scenes.without_scale`.
4. Keep the Pillow fallbacks (`captions_engine`, `board_engine`, `custom_engine`).
5. Update unit tests; keep the T5.2c baseline test and the T5.2d sanitiser tests.

## Acceptance
- Same frames as before within SSIM 0.98 on the T5.x comparison kits.
- Reviewer timings on the owner's project copy (one picture, `preview_segment`):
  essay no slower than 9.5 s, board no slower than 7.3 s.
