# T2.5 Migration: outro and chapter cards
Phase 2 · Size M · Depends on: T2.4

## Goal
The end card and the chapter card exist as scene templates, selectable per
channel, visually at least as good as the Pillow versions, and board and
custom-slide formats finally get a chapter card.

## Why
Smallest real migration: proves fonts, variables, palette and timing end to
end. Also closes `NEXT_STEPS.md` §3 (no chapter cards on board/custom)
without any Pillow work.

## Read first
- `app/outro.py`: the templates (`_render_twoup`, `_marquee`, quiet variants;
  read how each lays out title, subscribe hint, next-video slot).
- `app/titles.py`: `render_chapter` (kicker, title, accent; its timing is in
  `_render_segment`).
- `app/cuts/kit.py`: `chapter_screen` (the Pillow chapter for drawn cuts).
- `app/render.py`: where the chapter card is composed for the photographic
  path, and where board/custom return early without one.
- `static/scene/_base.html`, `runtime.js` presets (T2.2, T2.3).
- `FrameSpec` numbers: `arrive_s`, `exit_ratio`, `last_line_min_s`.

## Steps
1. **Outro.** `static/scene/cards/outro_twoup.html` and `outro_quiet.html`.
   Layout with CSS grid in the title-safe box; title via `data-var-text="title"`;
   accent from `var(--accent)`; entrance with `data-anim="rise"`; the card
   lands by `arrive_s` and then **does not move** (banned: title moves after
   landing). Duration from the outro spec (same seconds the Pillow version
   uses).
2. Register them: wherever the UI lists outro templates (search
   `outro` in `app/main.py` and `static/app.js`), add `scene:outro_twoup` and
   `scene:outro_quiet` with the same preview endpoint behaviour (T2.4 branch).
3. **Chapter card.** `static/scene/cards/chapter.html`: kicker (small caps,
   dim), title (one or two lines, 6 to 9 % of frame height), accent rule.
   Fade-in `arrive_s`, hold, fade-out `arrive_s * exit_ratio`.
4. **Board and custom formats**: in `board.render_segment` and the custom
   style path, when `chapter` is set, render the chapter card clip via
   `scenes.ensure_clip` and composite it over the last `hold` seconds of the
   segment with one extra ffmpeg pass (`overlay` with `enable='between(t,…)'`
   plus a fade). Keep it simple; T5.2a removes it for board segments.
5. **Photographic path**: leave `titles.render_chapter` PNG in place (it is
   cheap) but read its timing numbers from the FrameSpec (if T1.4 already did
   this, nothing to do).
6. Side-by-side check: render the Pillow outro and the scene outro for the
   same channel at 480×270 (`scene-preview` and the existing channel preview
   endpoint), save both PNGs under `docs/tickets/02-scene-engine/compare/`
   and look at them. Expect a designed difference, not a copy.

## Acceptance
- A channel with `scene:outro_twoup` shows the scene card in the channel
  preview and at the end of a Timeline proxy.
- A board-format project with a chapter mark shows a card in the proxy of
  the segment before it.

## Implementer notes
*Added 2026-10-26.*

- Registry: `outro.TEMPLATES` (outro.py:48) is a list of dicts with
  `key, label, max_slots, ground, ...`. Add
  `{"key": "scene:outro_twoup", "label": "Two up (scene)", "max_slots": 2, "ground": "dark", "scene": True}`
  and the quiet one. Read the `twoup` and `quiet` entries' other fields and
  copy them. `wants_backdrop()` must answer for scene keys too.
- What the Pillow outro is given: `render_outro(channel, video_title,
  out_path, backdrop, progress)`; the "next video" slots are drawn as
  placeholder plates (YouTube overlays the real end-screen elements on top
  of them). The scene must leave those plates in **exactly** the same
  pixel rectangles, or the creator's YouTube end-screen elements will not
  line up. Read the rectangle math in the twoup renderer and expose it as
  CSS variables from `scenes.render_card` rather than re-deriving it in CSS.
- Length: `channel.outro.duration` (5-20 s). The scene clip must be exactly
  that many frames; music fade is handled by `_audio_graph`, not the card.
- **Board/custom chapter overlay** (step 4): `board.render_segment` and
  `_render_custom_style` write `out` themselves. Post-process in
  `render._render_segment` after they return, so neither module changes:
  ```python
  if chapter and seg_len > hold + 0.2:
      card = scenes.ensure_clip(project, "cards/chapter.html", ctx, seconds=hold, size=size, fps=fps)
      _overlay_tail(out, card, start=seg_len - hold)   # one ffmpeg pass, -c:a none
  ```
  `_overlay_tail`: `[0:v][1:v]overlay=enable='gte(t,{start})':eof_action=pass`
  with the card input delayed by `-itsoffset {start}`. Render to a temp file
  and `os.replace`. The chapter card scene should have an opaque background
  (it is full-frame), so no alpha is needed; capturing alpha via CDP is a
  rabbit hole (`Emulation.setDefaultBackgroundColorOverride` + PNG with
  alpha + an alpha-capable intermediate codec). Avoid it.
- `compare/` PNGs: commit them (small, quarter size). They are the review
  record.
